# Kill criteria

Seven numbers a holder can read from Constellation's quarterly release or the listed subsidiaries' releases. Each names the line, threshold, window, current reading and what a breach means. None is a price test.

## 1. FX adjusted organic growth

Line: the organic growth sentence in the quarterly release, the figure after adjusting for foreign exchange. Threshold: zero or below. Window: four consecutive quarters. Current: 1 percent in Q2 2026 and 1 percent for H1 2026, against 4 percent in Q2 2025 and 3 percent for FY2025 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2025] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025]. Two quarters in already. Meaning: the installed base is shrinking in real terms and all growth must be bought. The closest thing to a single kill number.

## 2. Maintenance organic growth

Line: the maintenance and recurring revenue organic growth figure in the same release. Threshold: below 2 percent. Window: four consecutive quarters. Current: 6 percent for FY2025 [ledger:unmapped:FY2025 maintenance and recurring revenue organic growth], with maintenance at 76.6 percent of revenue [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026]. Meaning: maintenance organic is substantially price, so decay here with unit counts holding means the escalator has broken, the pricing power layer the first report called most exposed. Watch it before headline organic: it moves first and acquired mix cannot dress it up.

## 3. Deployment against return

Lines: acquisitions cash consideration, FCFA2S, and the stated reinvestment rate. Threshold: deployment above 1.0 times FCFA2S while FCFA2S per share growth stays below 8 percent. Window: two consecutive years. Current: 0.94 times in FY2025 and 2.12 in Q2 2026 [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:Q2 2026], a reinvestment rate of 89.9 percent for 2025 [ledger:unmapped:Reinvestment rate:2023, 2024, 2025], and third party ROIC of 10.97 percent against a stated 25 percent hurdle [ledger:unmapped:ROIC:2025, June 2026, TTM 2026] [ledger:unmapped:Hurdle rate IRR target:2024-2026]. Meaning: spending more to produce less is the failure mode of a serial acquirer, and looks like growth until it does not. Pair the two lines or the test is worthless.

## 4. FCFA2S per share growth

Lines: FCFA2S and the share count. Threshold: below 8 percent. Window: three consecutive years. Current: 54.74, 69.47 and 79.42 per share across FY2023 to FY2025 [metrics:fcfa2s_per_share:FY2023] [metrics:fcfa2s_per_share:FY2024] [metrics:fcfa2s_per_share:FY2025], on a share count flat at 21.19 million since FY2023 [ledger:shares_outstanding:FY2023] [ledger:shares_outstanding:Q2 2026]. Meaning: this is the compounding itself. The ten year case needs 8 to 10 percent a year [hold:hold_grid_10yr.entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:cagr 0.08 exit 25x]; three years below 8 makes the arithmetic fail even at a 25 times exit.

## 5. Leverage

Definition: net debt, total debt less cash as reported, over LTM FCFA2S, with the IRGA liability added as debt like. Threshold: above 2.5 times. Window: two consecutive quarters. Current: 1.20 times at FY2024 and 1.34 at Q1 2026 [metrics:net_debt_to_fcfa2s:FY2024] [metrics:net_debt_to_fcfa2s:Q1 2026], with an IRGA balance of 1,186 at H1 2026 [ledger:unmapped:IRGA liability balance and revaluation:H1 2026, Dec 2025]. Ignore the 6.38 times printed for Q2 2026, which divides by one quarter of FCFA2S [metrics:net_debt_to_fcfa2s:Q2 2026]. Meaning: leverage that high on 1 percent organic growth removes the option to keep buying through a downturn, when returns are best.

## 6. Leadership and governance

Lines: management change releases and the annual circular. Threshold: departure of the Constellation President, or of two operating group CEOs, inside four quarters, or any change to the stated hurdle rate. Window: four quarters. Current: Mark Miller has been President since late 2025 [holdings:volaris:leadership], four leadership successions have completed since 2023, and Perseus still has no publicly named successor CEO [holdings:perseus:gaps]. Meaning: the hurdle is the discipline, so a cut to it is management saying the returns are gone. Clustered exits say the bench cannot run itself.

## 7. Topicus organic growth

Line: the organic growth figure in the Topicus release. Threshold: FX adjusted organic below 2 percent. Window: four consecutive quarters. Current: 4 percent in Q2 2026, 4 for FY2025, 5 for FY2024 [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026] [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.organic_growth_pct:FY2025] [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.organic_growth_pct:FY2024]. Meaning: Topicus is the only part of the group with a repeated, clearly positive organic figure, in the most regulated verticals, so erosion shows here last. Lumine is the opposite control at negative 2 percent FX adjusted in Q1 2026 [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026], warning early but proving little alone.

## Monitoring routine

Each quarter read four documents in order. First the Constellation release: the organic growth sentence, taking the FX adjusted figure rather than the headline, then maintenance organic, then acquisitions cash consideration against FCFA2S for the quarter and trailing year. Second the Topicus release, for the organic figure, the clean read on the regulated base. Third the Lumine release, for FX adjusted organic, the early warning. Fourth, once a year, the annual release and circular for the reinvestment rate, share count, balance sheet and any change to the hurdle or group leadership. Three rules make it work. Use the FX adjusted series, since currency carried about two points of the 2026 headline, 3 percent reported against 1 percent adjusted in Q2 2026 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2026]. Never read deployment without a return beside it. And treat any figure not traceable to a company release, including every group level revenue number behind this analysis, as commentary rather than evidence.
