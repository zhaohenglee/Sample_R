# Jonas Software

Private operating group of Constellation Software, based in Ontario, Canada, buying small vertical software businesses in clubs, hospitality, fitness, construction and foodservice since 2003 [holdings:jonas:status_note].

## 1. What it does

Customers are owner operated and member owned: clubs, gyms, hotels, restaurants, contractors, plus thin niches such as marinas, salons and moving and storage [holdings:jonas:verticals]. Jonas reports 185 brands [holdings:jonas:business_count] in 40 verticals [holdings:jonas:vertical_count] and 30 countries [holdings:jonas:country_count] with 6,000 employees [holdings:jonas:employees], weighted to North America, the United Kingdom and Europe [holdings:jonas:geographies].

Scale evidence is thin. The only revenue figure is 840.2 USD million for 2024 and it is third party [holdings:jonas:revenue]. On it Jonas is 8.3 percent of Constellation FY2024 revenue [hold:concentration.revenue_share.jonas:FY2024] and 9.4 percent of parent headcount [hold:concentration.headcount_share.jonas:~2024], the latter resting on a parent headcount of 64,000 graded low confidence [holdings:parent_long_run:headcount:~2024]. Share of parent business unit count is a gap [hold:concentration.business_count_share.jonas:none].

## 2. Function in the whole

Primarily a high velocity deployer at the small end of the funnel: 29 acquisitions in 2024 [holdings:jonas:acquisitions_per_year:2024], 15 in that first half [holdings:jonas:acquisitions_per_year:2024_H1], more than 175 since 2003 [stage1:jonas.json:https://jonassoftware.com/about-us]. Nothing here suggests large deals, the opposite of Harris and Perseus.

Also an incubator of structure: CORA Group launched in November 2022 to add closing and onboarding capacity and carries over 150 brands [stage1:jonas.json:https://jonassoftware.com/jonas-software-launches-cora-group]; club and hospitality runs as Vertus Group [holdings:jonas:leadership]. It generates cash too, but that is inference, since no Jonas cash flow figure exists [holdings:jonas:gaps]. Not a geographic arm, not a listed currency. The main function is small deal throughput, the part of the engine showing the bottom of the funnel still clears the hurdle.

## 4. How it deploys capital

Count is well evidenced, price is not. 2024 set a record at 29 deals, 19 advisor led rather than proprietary, which normally raises price [stage1:jonas.json:https://jonassoftware.com/2024-year-in-review]; 2025 fell to 10 announced [stage1:jonas.json:https://jonassoftware.com/2025-year-in-review]. The same 2024 source gives an average of 4.4 deals a year for 2020 to 2025 [holdings:jonas:acquisitions_per_year:average_2020_2025], irreconcilable with either print, so the cadence series is inconsistent.

Typical size is a gap and Jonas states no hurdle of its own [holdings:jonas:gaps]. What applies is the parent's tiered target, reported third party as 30 percent IRR below 1 USD million of acquired revenue against 20 percent above 4 USD million [holdings:parent_long_run:hurdle_rate:long-run], so the group buying the smallest assets owes the highest return. Parent ROIC on a third party series was 10.97 percent annualised in Q2 2026 [holdings:parent_long_run:roic_pct:Q2 2026 (annualized)], and no group return is disclosed, so Jonas cannot be placed against the parent.

## 5. AI exposure map

Jonas is more exposed than Harris, structurally rather than speculatively: almost none of its customers has a regulator demanding a certified authoritative record.

System of record and regulatory data, least exposed, beyond ten years: a minority slice only, being long term care, health care, case management and legal software [holdings:jonas:verticals], of unknown revenue weight since no split by vertical exists [holdings:jonas:gaps].

Switching cost and inertia, least eroded in the first report's framework, beyond ten years: the strongest part of Jonas. Dues billing, member management, job costing and fitness payments sit in the money path, and migrating a live billing system stays painful however cheap a replacement gets.

Embedded workflow and integration, partly exposed, three to seven years: hotel booking and distribution, which is Bookassist [holdings:jonas:notable_businesses]. Agents do that work well.

Pricing power and thin niches, most exposed, two to five years on price and a medium horizon on niche entry: Jonas carries the most weight here of any group. Average revenue per brand is 4.5 USD million, from 840.2 USD million across 185 brands, computed here [holdings:jonas:revenue] [holdings:jonas:business_count]. That is the size the first report flagged once build cost collapses, and a price escalator in a marina or salon has no compliance argument behind it. The counterweight is parent level only: DerbySoft went at 4.0 times revenue, so private prices are not repricing for obsolescence, a figure from the first report's intake rather than this one [stage1:capital_allocation:https://www.mbi-deepdives.com/csu2q26/].

No Jonas organic growth figure exists; the only comment is the parent's 2 percent for 2024 [holdings:jonas:organic_growth_commentary], against 1 percent FX adjusted in H1 2026 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026] with maintenance at 76.6 percent of revenue [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026]. The one group AI statement is Jonas Fitness committing millions to AI development, with no product detail [holdings:jonas:ai_statements].

## 6. Leadership

Barry Symons has been CEO since 2007, after 11 years at Constellation including CFO from 2004 to 2007 [holdings:jonas:leadership], the longest unbroken tenure among these groups. The bench is internally grown: Matt Otchet arrived through the Clubsystems acquisition in 2005 and now runs CORA Group, and Jim Fedigan became President of Jonas Club Software in 2011 and now runs club and hospitality [holdings:jonas:leadership]. Not known: any succession event, named successor to Symons, or allocator exported to another group [holdings:jonas:gaps]. Jonas grows sub group leaders inside itself and, on this evidence, none for the wider system.

## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate. The function is durable: small deal throughput against a 30 percent hurdle tier at the small end [holdings:parent_long_run:hurdle_rate:long-run] does not need another large asset, and CORA shows capacity built rather than awaited [holdings:jonas:status_note]. Leadership depth is the best of any private group here [holdings:jonas:leadership]. But the verticals sit higher on the exposure ladder than Harris's, average brand revenue of 4.5 USD million computed above leaves little room to absorb price compression, and the evidence is one third party revenue figure, one disclosed deal price, and no organic growth, cash flow or group return [holdings:jonas:gaps].

The number that would change this verdict: Jonas's own year in review acquisition count at 10 or fewer for three consecutive years, after 29 in 2024 [holdings:jonas:acquisitions_per_year:2024] and 10 in 2025 [stage1:jonas.json:https://jonassoftware.com/2025-year-in-review]. Two further years there mean the small deal funnel, the group's whole function, has closed. A rebound above 20 deals [holdings:jonas:acquisitions_per_year:2024] restores strong.
