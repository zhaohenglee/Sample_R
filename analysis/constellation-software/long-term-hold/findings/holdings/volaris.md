Private operating group of Constellation Software, generalist by design, and the incubator of every new Constellation branded portfolio of the last four years [holdings:volaris:status_note].

Capital deployer, well evidenced: 28 acquisitions in 2023 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2023], 40 or more in 2024 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2024], 30 in 2025 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2025] and 14 in H1 2026 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:H1 2026], so 112 deals in three and a half years, computed here, a cadence no other group in this ledger discloses.

Incubator of new groups, the distinctive function. Vencora launched in November 2022 for banking and insurance [stage1:volaris.json:https://www.volarisgroup.com/press-room/volaris-group-launches-vencora/]. Lumine, the former Volaris communications and media portfolio, spun out in February 2023 with WideOrbit [holdings:lumine:status_note]. Omegro launched on 25 June 2024 and still reports inside Volaris [holdings:omegro:status_note], not 2023 as brief.json assumed. Modaxo is the people transportation portfolio [holdings:volaris:notable_businesses:Modaxo].

Listed currency, indirectly: the Lumine spin out turned a Volaris portfolio into a stake worth 5.3 to 6.0 percent of Constellation market capitalisation [hold:look_through.lumine.share_of_csu_market_cap_reported_cad:low] [hold:look_through.lumine.share_of_csu_market_cap_reported_cad:high] on a 61.4 percent economic interest [holdings:lumine:csu_economic_interest_pct].

Talent bench is section 6. Cash generator is unproven: no usable revenue, margin or cash flow figure [holdings:volaris:gaps].

The most important function is incubator: Volaris is the only group here that manufactures new deployers and hands them a portfolio or a ticker.

Least exposed, system of record and regulatory data, beyond ten years: transit, justice, public safety, government administration, library and cultural collections, aviation, digital banking [holdings:volaris:verticals]. Parent maintenance revenue at 76.6 percent of total in H1 2026 is not displacement [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026]. Most exposed, pricing power and thin niches, two to five years: 240 companies across 40 verticals averages 6.0 per vertical, computed here, so the typical unit is small and cheap to contest, marine, rental management and agri food most of all [holdings:volaris:verticals]. Volaris AI activity is process, not product: a Center of Excellence from late 2024, an AI Accelerator for 2026 [holdings:volaris:ai_statements], no product launch on record [holdings:volaris:gaps].

## 6. Leadership

Mike Dufton has been CEO since January 2024, after more than a decade inside Volaris and founding Vencora in 2022, which he still runs [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mike Dufton]. The person running 240 businesses also runs a division inside them.

Mark Miller is Executive Chairman of Volaris and President of Constellation Software [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mark Miller]. He co founded Trapeze in 1988, moved to Executive Chairman in January 2024, became Constellation President in late 2025 and chairs the Lumine board [holdings:lumine:leadership:Mark Miller]. This is the most consequential leadership fact in the ledger. Constellation's capital allocation now runs through a Volaris operator, and the first report flagged that hurdle discipline is untested without Mark Leonard, who left the board in May 2026. The January 2024 Volaris handover now reads as the rehearsal.

Allocators produced for others: David Nyland joined Volaris in 2014 to build what became Lumine, overseeing more than 30 acquisitions since [holdings:lumine:leadership:David Nyland]; Brian Beattie is CFO of Volaris and sits on the Lumine board [holdings:lumine:leadership:Brian Beattie]; Kevin Bradley and Troy O'Connor are co CEOs of Omegro [holdings:volaris:leadership:Kevin Bradley] [holdings:volaris:leadership:Troy O'Connor]. Four allocator seats from one group in four years is bench evidence, not a claim.

Not known: Jeff Chow's tenure [holdings:volaris:leadership:Jeff Chow], the Omegro co CEOs' backgrounds, incentives, and bench depth below the five named. A Constellation page still lists Miller as Volaris CEO [holdings:lumine:leadership:Mark Miller] against the announcement naming Dufton [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mike Dufton].

## 7. Durability verdict

Strong, on structure rather than on numbers. What matters is not that Volaris buys software companies but that it keeps producing the people and vehicles that buy them: Vencora, Lumine, Omegro, Modaxo, and Constellation's President [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mark Miller]. That function depends least on any single vertical, and there are 40 [holdings:volaris:vertical_count]. The qualification is real: no Volaris financial statement exists, the only revenue figures are a live conflict [holdings:conflicts:volaris:revenue], and margin, cash flow and organic growth are gaps [holdings:volaris:gaps].

The number that would change this verdict: Volaris annual acquisition count below 20 for two consecutive calendar years, against 28 in 2023 and 30 in 2025 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2023] [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], observable in the group's own year in review disclosure on a two year window.
