# Omegro

Branded portfolio inside Volaris Group, not a separate Constellation operating group, launched on June 25, 2024 from Volaris businesses in supply chain, ERP, fleet and asset software [holdings:omegro:status_note].

## 1. What it does

More than 30 business units [holdings:omegro:business_count], about 2,000 employees [holdings:omegro:employees] and more than 70 countries [holdings:omegro:country_count], centred on North America, Europe and Australasia [holdings:omegro:geographies]. Verticals are warehousing and inventory control, ERP and CRM, fleet and logistics, asset maintenance and process automation, narrowed in January 2026 to Enterprise Asset Management [holdings:omegro:status_note]. Revenue, margin and organic growth are gap, so revenue share of the parent is gap [hold:concentration:revenue_share:omegro]. Headcount is the only tie to scale: 3.1 percent of Constellation on a low confidence parent figure [hold:concentration:headcount_share:omegro:~2024], and 11.4 percent of the 17,500 employees across the Volaris portfolio [holdings:omegro:employees] [holdings:volaris:employees:across_portfolio_2025], computed here.

## 2. Function in the whole

Omegro is an incubator of a new group and a talent bench. It is not a verifiable cash generator, a geographic arm or a listed currency. Its value to a holder is as evidence, because it is the decentralisation thesis running in the open on a short clock. A coherent portfolio was carved out of a 240 company generalist group [holdings:volaris:business_count], given two co CEOs split between capital generation and capital deployment [holdings:omegro:leadership:Kevin Bradley] [holdings:omegro:leadership:Troy O'Connor] and its own CFO [holdings:omegro:leadership:Katie Mansell], and marketed to founders as a permanent home that never resells a business [stage1:omegro.json:https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/06/25/2903650/0/en/Introducing-Omegro-A-Global-Collective-of-Technology-Businesses-Offering-A-Permanent-and-Safe-Home-to-Provide-Long-Term-Sustainable-Growth-for-the-World-s-Most-Impactful-Software-C.html]. Those are allocator seats that did not exist before, which is the part of the thesis that matters over ten years. It is weak positive evidence, not proof, because no Omegro return is observable.

Does it look like a future spin out? Structurally yes, financially not yet. Lumine was spun out of the same group in February 2023 [holdings:lumine:status_note] and Vencora was launched as a Volaris division in 2022 [holdings:volaris:notable_businesses:Vencora]. But Lumine listed with FY2023 revenue of 499.7 USD millions, 5.9 percent of Constellation revenue [hold:concentration:revenue_share:lumine:FY2023], paired with the WideOrbit anchor deal that needed a listed currency. Omegro's deals are small and undisclosed, and the January 2026 narrowing to Enterprise Asset Management [holdings:omegro:status_note] reads either as shaping ahead of a standalone identity or as the original multi vertical brand not holding together. Possible inside five years, unsupported on this evidence.

## 4. How it deploys capital

Six of the seven recorded businesses were branded Omegro deals between November 2024 and September 2025 [holdings:omegro:notable_businesses]. Every price is undisclosed, so deal size and returns are gap [holdings:omegro:gaps]. No Omegro hurdle is disclosed; the tiered 30, 25 and 20 percent IRR bands are a third party account of Constellation and Volaris policy [holdings:volaris:hurdle_rate]. Volaris closed more than 40 acquisitions in 2024 and 30 in 2025 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2024] [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], so Omegro is a small slice of group volume, with no shift to larger deals.

## 5. AI exposure map

Omegro leans to the exposed end of the Constellation mix. Beyond ten years, least exposed: municipal ERP and asset registry data on system of record, and the warehousing, inventory control and ERP installed bases on switching cost and inertia. Three to seven years, partly exposed: fleet, telematics and process automation on embedded workflow. Two to five years, most exposed: retail inventory planning, RFID and small hospitality niches on pricing power and thin niches, where forecasting is the prediction problem models attack first. No Omegro AI statement exists [holdings:omegro:ai_statements]; the only cover is Volaris level, an AI Center of Excellence from late 2024 and an AI Accelerator in 2026 [holdings:volaris:ai_statements].

## 6. Leadership

Kevin Bradley and Troy O'Connor have been co CEOs since launch, Bradley on capital generation, O'Connor on capital deployment [holdings:omegro:leadership:Kevin Bradley] [holdings:omegro:leadership:Troy O'Connor]. O'Connor joined in 2017 as CEO of Smartrak when it was acquired [holdings:omegro:leadership:Troy O'Connor], a founder turned allocator, which is the succession pattern the thesis predicts. Katie Mansell is CFO, prior Volaris and SpecTec, start date unknown [holdings:omegro:leadership:Katie Mansell]. Not known: board, oversight, reporting line, and whether the co CEO split is permanent [holdings:omegro:gaps].

## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate. The function is durable, because the parent has carved out new groups repeatedly and Lumine shows the path can end in a listed entity [holdings:lumine:status_note]. Omegro itself carries no part of the hold case: at 3.1 percent of parent headcount [hold:concentration:headcount_share:omegro:~2024] with no revenue disclosure it cannot move the engine, and its verticals lean toward the two most exposed AI layers. Read it as evidence about the structure, not as a source of value.

The number that would change this verdict: Omegro branded acquisition announcements, six between November 2024 and September 2025 [holdings:omegro:notable_businesses]. Fewer than three in each of two consecutive calendar years, or the brand folded back into Volaris, would say incubation stalls after launch and would weaken the decentralisation case one notch.

- Organic growth, so Omegro cannot be set against the parent's 1 percent FX adjusted H1 2026 reading [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026].
