## 5. AI exposure map

**System of record and regulatory data, least exposed, beyond ten years.** Most of Harris sits here. Utility billing, local government finance, public safety, courts and the clinical chart are statutory records of authority. No parent number suggests displacement: maintenance revenue rose to 76.6 percent of the total by H1 2026 from 71.2 percent in FY2023 [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026] [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2023].

**Switching cost and inertia, least eroded.** Replacing a municipal or utility system of record needs a tender, a council vote and a multi year migration, and cheaper software does not shorten that. This is judgment: no Harris churn figure exists [holdings:harris:gaps].

**Embedded workflow and integration, partly exposed, three to seven years.** Healthcare interoperability and data integrity work is the data movement labour agents do well. Harris automates it itself with an Intelligent MPI Triage Solution on AI driven logic and human review [holdings:harris:ai_statements], which defends margin and shrinks the services line at once. The headcount decline from 25,607 to 24,714 is the shape that takes [holdings:harris:employees:2024] [holdings:harris:employees:2025].

**Pricing power and thin niches, most exposed, two to five years.** Harris's small public sector units are the classic niche with no funded competitor. Public buyers are slow, but escalators leak first. Parent FX adjusted organic growth of 1 percent in H1 2026 against 4 percent in Q2 2025 is the symptom, unsplit by group [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2025].

The real exposure at Harris is not AI. It is Altera. An acute care health record losing share to a broader integrated rival needs no agents to shrink. This dataset holds no Altera revenue, retention or hospital count, and no mention of Epic, so that read is my judgment, not evidence. The price is the evidence: 0.75 times revenue buys a base to harvest, not grow. Third party commentary calls Harris growth acquisition skewed, with integration strain [holdings:harris:organic_growth_commentary].

## 6. Leadership

Jeff Bender ran Harris for roughly two decades after CFO and COO roles, then became Executive Chairman in June 2024 [holdings:harris:leadership:Jeff Bender]. Jean Soucy took over as Group CEO on 1 June 2024 [stage1:harris:https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/harris-operating-group-of-constellation-software-announces-executive-changes], having joined in 2012 as Public Sector Group President [holdings:harris:leadership:Jean Soucy].

The succession was internal, on twelve years of tenure, and kept the predecessor in the building, the shape the parent reused a year later. It went to the public sector side of the house, not healthcare, which says where the centre of gravity sits. And Harris has not exported an allocator upward: Miller came from Volaris [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mark Miller], as did the newer collectives [holdings:volaris:notable_businesses]. Not known: when Bender joined [holdings:harris:gaps], who runs Altera, whether Soucy has a successor.

## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate, and the closest of the private groups to strong. The durable part is deployment: 270 or more deals over fifty years [holdings:harris:acquisitions_per_year:since_1976] and 24 then 19 in the last two disclosed years [holdings:harris:acquisitions_per_year:2024] [holdings:harris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], in verticals on the least exposed layer. Against it, roughly 40.0 percent of Constellation's people [hold:concentration:headcount_share:harris:~2024] sit in a group with no disclosed revenue, cash flow, organic growth or return figure. The structural case is good and the financial case is unverifiable, which is why this is not strong.

The number that would change this verdict: the acquisition count in Harris's own annual year in review, 19 for 2025 [holdings:harris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], below 10 for two consecutive years. That would mean the group had stopped replacing its ageing base, and would move this to weak.
