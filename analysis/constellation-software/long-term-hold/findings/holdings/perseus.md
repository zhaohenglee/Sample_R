Private operating group of Constellation Software, based in Markham, Ontario, founded 2004. Buys and holds vertical software for mortgage finance, dealerships, homebuilding and real estate [holdings:perseus:status_note].

Perseus runs 70 or more businesses [holdings:perseus:business_count] across 12 or more verticals [holdings:perseus:vertical_count]. Headcount is 6,000 or more [holdings:perseus:employees], third party and in unresolved conflict with 3,900 [holdings:perseus:employees], about 9.4 percent of parent headcount in 2024 on a low confidence denominator [hold:concentration:headcount_share:perseus:~2024].

## 2. Function in the whole

Capital deployer, and the group that does large structured carve outs. The Black Knight purchase at 700 million USD was mostly seller paper, only 200 million USD of it cash [holdings:perseus:largest_deal_price], nearly matching the parent's entire FY2023 acquisition cash line of 732 million USD [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2023] while the cash out was 27 percent of that year's parent deployment (200 and 732, computed here). Harris paid a similar headline price for Altera in 2022 [holdings:harris:notable_businesses:Altera Digital Health], but in cash, not forty year paper.

Cash generator cannot be assessed, group cash flow being gap [holdings:perseus:gaps]. Neither a geographic arm nor a listed currency. Talent bench evidence is thin: Bonnie Wilhelm rose from CFO of a portfolio company to co president [holdings:perseus:leadership], and no group has been carved out of Perseus, unlike Volaris [holdings:volaris:status_note]. Most important function: the large deal arm.

## 4. How it deploys capital

Recorded deal counts are two in 2020, two in 2023 and three in 2024 [holdings:perseus:acquisitions_per_year:2020] [holdings:perseus:acquisitions_per_year:2023] [holdings:perseus:acquisitions_per_year:2024], from one third party tracker and incomplete. No Perseus hurdle or IRR statement is disclosed [holdings:perseus:gaps]; the only one on file is the parent's tiered 20 to 30 percent target, midpoint 25 percent, itself third party [holdings:parent_long_run:hurdle_rate:long-run].

The forty year seller note is where hurdle discipline gets decided. It lowers the real price: interest runs 35 million USD a year (500 and 7 percent, computed here), and discounted at the parent's own 25 percent hurdle an interest only note of that shape is worth about 140 million USD, so the economic cost sits near 340 million USD not 700 million USD (500 at 7 percent for 40 years discounted at 25 percent, computed here). The schedule is a gap [holdings:perseus:gaps], so that is direction, not valuation.

It also flatters the hurdle: a fixed 7 percent against a 20 to 30 percent target leaves 13 to 23 points of spread (7 against 20 and 30, computed here), so a deal can clear on structure while the unlevered return is mediocre. No unlevered or group return is disclosed [holdings:perseus:gaps]; the parent series of 25 percent ROIC in FY2010 [holdings:parent_long_run:roic_pct:FY2010] against 10.97 percent at Q2 2026 [holdings:parent_long_run:roic_pct:Q2 2026 (annualized)] mixes providers and definitions.

The note is also real value: permanent capital at a known cost, eroded by inflation. The open question is whether the structure gets repeated where the unlevered economics do not stand up, and nothing here lets an outsider check. ## 6. Leadership

Bonnie Wilhelm has been co president since 2022 and chief operating officer, after ten years as CFO at Constellation HomeBuilder Systems [holdings:perseus:leadership], a LinkedIn sourced record. The board holds Salna, Mark Leonard and Constellation chief investment officer Bernie Anzarouth [holdings:perseus:leadership], and Leonard leaves the Constellation board on 15 May 2026 [holdings:perseus:leadership]. ## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate. The verticals are well placed, the loan record and the dealer spine sitting in the two least exposed layers, and it closes assets few groups can. What blocks a strong verdict is the absence of numbers: no group revenue, cash flow, organic growth, hurdle or return disclosure, and no named CEO three years after the founder left. A group whose signature move is converting price into forty years of cheap term is one whose hurdle arithmetic an outsider cannot audit.

The number that would change this verdict: announced Perseus acquisitions falling to zero across four consecutive quarters, against three in calendar 2024 [holdings:perseus:acquisitions_per_year:2024], would move this to weak, cadence being the group's only observable output.
