# Topicus.com Inc.

Listed subsidiary, TSX: TOI [holdings:topicus:type], Netherlands based. Constellation's European acquisition machine: controlled by vote, minority owned by economics, reporting in a currency the parent ledger cannot convert.

## 1. What it does

European vertical market software sold mostly to public bodies and regulated professions, through three operating groups, TSS Blue, TSS Public and the Topicus Operating Group. Over 100,000 customers, 40 verticals, 26 countries and roughly 10,000 employees, the Netherlands 49 percent of revenue and France 17 percent, all stage1 only [stage1:topicus.json:https://topicus.com/about-us]. The ledger counts 13 verticals in 16 countries [holdings:topicus:verticals] [holdings:topicus:geographies].

FY2025 revenue was 1,552.3 EUR millions [holdings:topicus:revenue:FY2025], up 19.9 percent after 15.1 percent in FY2024 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2025] [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2024], with H1 2026 up 20.2 percent [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:revenue_growth_yoy:H1 2026]. FY2025 FCFA2S was 218.7 EUR millions, a 14.1 percent margin [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:fcfa2s_margin:FY2025]. Maintenance and recurring revenue is roughly 70 percent, third party [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:maintenance_share_of_revenue].

## 2. Function in the whole

Geographic arm first, listed currency second, capital deployer third. Not a cash generator: no distribution to Constellation appears in these files.

The listed currency function works in two senses that pull opposite ways. In corporate finance it works: Topicus has its own stock at CAD 100.50 [holdings:topicus:share_price], a EUR 700 millions revolver to 2029 [stage1:topicus.json:https://seekingwinners.substack.com/p/topicus-q2-2025-earnings-brief], net debt of only 127.3 EUR millions [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:net_debt:Q2 2026], and in FY2025 deployed 1.79 times its FCFA2S [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025] with no cited capital call on the parent. In the reporting sense it costs the analysis: Topicus reports in EUR [holdings:topicus:revenue_currency] with no rate sourced, so the USD look through value and the parent revenue share are both null [hold:look_through:topicus:usd_value] [hold:concentration:revenue_share:topicus]. How much of Constellation is Europe is untestable.

Shareholders watch the 30 percent more than they capture it. Look through value is CAD 2,546.4 millions [hold:look_through:topicus:look_through_value_native], 3.91 percent of Constellation's market cap [hold:look_through:share_of_csu_market_cap:topicus:reported_cad:single] [ledger:market_cap_cad:2026-09-08], 3.93 to 4.25 percent across USDCAD 1.35 to 1.25 [hold:look_through:share_of_csu_market_cap:topicus:fx_sensitivity]. Constellation directs the whole engine through 50.1 percent of the votes [holdings:topicus:csu_voting_control] while its holders own four percent of it. Control that far above economics leaks value, offset only because the rest went to those same holders at the spin out [stage1:parent_long_run:https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/11/17/2128311/0/en/Constellation-Software-Inc-Provides-Update-in-Connection-with-Proposed-Topicus-com-Spin-Out-and-Previously-Declared-Conditional-Dividend.html]. A buyer today gets only the 30.35 percent, dated 2021-01-05 [holdings:topicus:csu_economic_interest_pct].

## 5. AI exposure map

System of record and regulatory data, least exposed, horizon beyond ten years absent regulators certifying model output. Education, healthcare, social services, government and legal services dominate the vertical list [holdings:topicus:verticals], so Topicus sits deeper in this layer than Constellation as a whole. Switching cost sits at the same horizon: migration cost, not build cost, is the barrier.

Embedded workflow and integration, partly exposed, three to seven years. The 30 percent that is not maintenance [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:maintenance_share_of_revenue] is licence, services and integration, the line agents hollow first, and Constellation's own maintenance share is higher at 76.6 percent [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026].

Pricing power and thin niches, most exposed, two to five years. Reported organic growth was 5 percent in FY2024 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:organic_growth_pct:FY2024] and 4 percent in FY2025 and Q2 2026 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:organic_growth_pct:FY2025] [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026], beating the parent's 1 percent FX adjusted [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026]. That comparison is invalid: Topicus publishes no FX adjusted series [holdings:topicus:gaps] while operating in seven non euro currencies [holdings:topicus:geographies]. A third party FY2023 figure of 22.72 percent was dropped as mislabelled [holdings:conflicts:topicus:organic_growth_pct FY2023]. No group AI statement exists [holdings:topicus:ai_statements].

## 6. Leadership

Robin van Poelje has been Chief Executive Officer since 2021 [holdings:topicus:leadership:Robin van Poelje]. He founded Total Specific Solutions and ran it from 2010 to 2020 [stage1:topicus.json:https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/11/25/2341279/0/en/Robin-van-Poelje-named-as-Chief-Executive-Officer-of-Topicus-com.html], fifteen years in the asset. He took over from Daan Dijkhuizen, who moved to Constellation VMS Ventures as Managing Partner while staying Group CEO of the Topicus Operating Group [holdings:topicus:leadership:Daan Dijkhuizen]: the talent bench runs toward the parent.

The board cuts the other way. Seven directors including Mark Leonard, Jeff Bender and Dijkhuizen did not stand for re election in May 2023 [stage1:topicus.json:https://topicus.com/news/topicuscom-inc-announces-re-election-of-members-of-the-board-of-the-board-of-directors], so Constellation withdrew its most senior names while keeping 50.1 percent of the votes [holdings:topicus:csu_voting_control]. Director tenures and backgrounds are unknown [holdings:topicus:leadership:John Billowits], and no succession plan is on file.

## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate. Its own stock, facility and balance sheet, 1.79 times own cash flow deployed [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025] and 19.9 percent revenue growth [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2025] in least exposed verticals make a sound function. Not strong, for three reasons. The economic interest is a January 2021 figure with nothing since [holdings:topicus:csu_economic_interest_pct]. Organic growth is reported only, never currency adjusted, so the metric that matters most sits on the friendlier basis. And FY2025 deployment was one minority stake in a listed Polish IT group, not a repeatable pipeline of small deals.

The number that would change this verdict: Topicus organic growth below 2 percent for four consecutive quarters, FX adjusted once published and reported until then, against 4 to 5 percent through Q2 2026 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026].
