# Lumine Group Inc.

Listed subsidiary on the TSX Venture Exchange as LMN [holdings:lumine:type], reporting in USD [holdings:lumine:revenue_currency]. Constellation's communications and media arm, carved out of Volaris in February 2023, still controlled and consolidated [holdings:lumine:status_note].

## 1. What it does

Verticals: carrier business and operational support systems, broadcast and media, print, and wireless 5G core and RAN software, sold to carriers, broadcasters and media owners [holdings:lumine:verticals]. Staff: 33 percent North America, 27 Europe ex UK, 23 Asia Pacific, 9 UK, 50 plus countries [holdings:lumine:geographies]. It claims 30 plus businesses, 1,000 customers and 3,500 employees, stage1 only [stage1:lumine:https://www.luminegroup.com/our-story/], against a third party count of 26 [holdings:lumine:gaps]. Revenue was 499.7 USD millions in FY2023 [holdings:lumine:revenue:FY2023], 668.4 in FY2024 [holdings:lumine:revenue:FY2024] and 765.7 in FY2025 [holdings:lumine:revenue:FY2025], growth of 33.8 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:lumine:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2024] then 14.6 percent [hold:listed_subsidiaries:lumine:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2025]. H1 2026 was 443.4 [holdings:lumine:revenue:H1 2026]. Lumine is 6.6 percent of Constellation revenue [hold:concentration:revenue_share:lumine:FY2025].

## 5. AI exposure map

Communications and media is the hardest vertical in the family to defend, because of the customer, not the software.

System of record and switching cost, least exposed. Carrier billing and charging are audited systems of record, not regulator certified, and agents cut the cost of building a replacement, not of migrating a live network. Horizon: beyond ten years.

Embedded workflow and integration, partly exposed. Broadcast traffic, ad sales, asset management and playout sit here, most of WideOrbit, Quortex and Imagine [holdings:lumine:notable_businesses:Imagine Communications], and carve outs arrive carrying services revenue, which agents attack first. Horizon: three to seven years.

Pricing power and thin niches, most exposed, and worse here than anywhere in the family. Roughly 1,000 accounts [stage1:lumine:https://www.luminegroup.com/our-story/] sit in two consolidating industries, so every carrier merger deletes a paying account and hands the survivor leverage at renewal; media owners face streaming compression and print declines [holdings:lumine:verticals]. That inverts Constellation's position of many small captive customers. Horizon: two to five years.

FX adjusted organic growth: 0 percent FY2023 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:lumine:organic_growth_pct:FY2023], negative 9 FY2024 [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2024], 1 FY2025 [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025], negative 2 Q1 2026 [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026], 1 Q2 2026 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:lumine:organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026]. Parent 3 percent FY2025 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025] and 1 H1 2026 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026]; Topicus 4 FY2025 [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:organic_growth_pct:FY2025]. Weakest organic record in the family, and the only clearly negative full year. Qualifier: FY2024 was the first full year after the big carve outs, so part of it is acquired runoff.

AI statements here are about cost and product, not price: a core capability claim and more than 600 augmentation use cases [holdings:lumine:ai_statements]. No Lumine specific attrition or pricing disclosure exists [holdings:lumine:gaps], and the CEO's claim of the best pre FX organic quarter in three years [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_commentary] sits oddly against Q1 2026's figures.

David Nyland founded the business, CEO and director since 2022, joined Volaris in 2014 to build it, more than 30 acquisitions overseen, earlier operating roles at Architel and Syndesis [holdings:lumine:leadership]. The deepest single person dependency in the group. Mary Anne Lavallee became CFO in May 2025, replacing Brian Beattie, now a director and Volaris CFO [holdings:lumine:leadership]. Mark Miller, parent President and COO and Volaris CEO, chairs the board, with Topicus CEO Robin van Poelje on it [holdings:lumine:leadership]. Not known: any successor to Nyland, the new CFO's background, board tenure dates [holdings:lumine:gaps]. No allocators produced for other groups; the flow runs the other way.

## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate. Deployment is durable and probably gets easier for a decade: vendors keep shedding non core software and Lumine has a repeatable process for it [holdings:lumine:notable_businesses:Nokia device management and service management platforms]. Almost no value comes from the installed base, organic growth between negative 9 and 1 percent over four years [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2024] [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025], so the case rests on buying well with no disclosed return to check. A group that must buy to grow, into a consolidating customer base, with no visible returns, cannot be graded strong.

The number that would change this verdict: FX adjusted organic growth at or below negative 2 percent for four consecutive quarters, which means the base shrinks faster than deals at 1.44 times FCFA2S [hold:listed_subsidiaries:lumine:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025] replace it.
