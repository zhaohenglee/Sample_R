# Vela Software

Private operating group of Constellation Software, wholly owned, no separate financials. Vertical market software for operators of physical assets and travel networks. The smallest of the five private groups.

Twenty one verticals, led by oil and gas, mining, construction and engineering, travel and education [holdings:vela:verticals], across eleven named geographies including Croatia, Australia, South Africa and South America [holdings:vela:geographies], and a stage1 claim of over 100 markets [stage1:vela:https://velasoftwaregroup.com/].

## 5. AI exposure map

Least exposed, beyond ten years: engineering document, interface and contract management for capital projects, a record carrying contractual and audit weight, meaning Coreworx and Scenario [holdings:vela:notable_businesses], with mine and reserve modelling a weaker version of the same anchor [holdings:vela:notable_businesses]. Switching cost also runs beyond ten years in travel connectivity: DerbySoft sits between hotels and distributors, a network position not a feature [stage1:capital_allocation:https://www.mbi-deepdives.com/csu2q26/]. Embedded workflow and integration, partly exposed at three to seven years, is most of Vela: booking engines and geoscience workbenches are integration heavy, and seismic interpretation is a direct machine learning target. Pricing power and thin niches are most exposed, two to five years: automotive, textile and apparel, retail, CPG and wholesale distribution have no regulatory floor [holdings:vela:verticals], and consumer travel is the one place in the portfolio where an agent already touches the end customer.

On mix Vela is the most AI exposed of the five: least regulated data, most travel, most technical interpretation. That judges verticals, not performance: the one Vela AI statement is generic [holdings:vela:ai_statements] and no group organic series exists to test it.

## 7. Durability verdict

Adequate, the lowest confidence verdict of the five because the evidence is thinnest. The positives: a settled CEO six years in who came through an acquisition [holdings:vela:leadership], thirteen businesses bought in 2024 [holdings:vela:notable_businesses], and a step up in deal size to CAD 400 million [stage1:capital_allocation:https://www.mbi-deepdives.com/csu2q26/]. None of it is verifiable against a financial number: with no revenue, organic growth, cash flow, business count or hurdle, Vela cannot be shown to earn its cost of capital, and its mix is the most AI exposed of the five. The headcount decline came through heavy buying, the shape of a base contracting underneath the deals.

The number that would change this verdict: third party headcount below 3,400, more than 10 percent under the March 2026 reading of 3,809, computed here [holdings:vela:employees:2026-03], held for four consecutive quarterly readings while Vela keeps announcing five or more acquisitions a year. It is the only continuous series here, and a fall while capital goes in means the base is shrinking faster than deals replace it.
