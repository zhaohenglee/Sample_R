# Constellation Software: Hold for Ten Years?

## Executive Summary

Verdict: Hold, with conditions.

Constellation qualifies as a ten year holding on its reinvestment engine and on the structure that runs it. It does not qualify on organic growth. This is a different answer from the first report's Not Buy, which was about price at CAD 2,827.94 [ledger:share_price_cad:2026-09-11]. A holder who already owns the business keeps it and watches seven numbers. A buyer at today's price still faces the first report's answer. The gap between the two verdicts is price, not the business.

The hold ends if the decisive criterion breaches: FCFA2S per share growth below 8 percent for three consecutive years. That fails the ten year arithmetic at any exit multiple up to 25 times [hold:hold_grid_10yr:entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:cagr 0.08 exit 25x]. Two of the other six criteria are already live. FX adjusted organic growth has been 1 percent for two consecutive quarters against a four quarter window at zero [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026]. Deployment ran 2.12 times FCFA2S in Q2 2026 while third party ROIC fell to 10.97 percent [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:Q2 2026] [ledger:unmapped:ROIC:2025, June 2026, TTM 2026].

Reasons that carry the call

1. The engine has delivered what the decade needs. Ten years of 8 to 10 percent FCFA2S per share growth at a 20 to 25 times exit returns 8.3 to 8.7 percent a year from the first report's entry [hold:hold_grid_10yr:entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:cagr 0.10 exit 20x] [hold:hold_grid_10yr:entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:cagr 0.08 exit 25x] and 9.2 to 9.7 percent from the FX corrected entry [hold:hold_grid_10yr:entry_usd_2080_29_at_usdcad_1_3594:cagr 0.10 exit 20x] [hold:hold_grid_10yr:entry_usd_2080_29_at_usdcad_1_3594:cagr 0.08 exit 25x]. The record sits above that band. FCFA2S per share went from 54.74 to 69.47 to 79.42 across FY2023 to FY2025 [metrics:fcfa2s_per_share:FY2023] [metrics:fcfa2s_per_share:FY2024] [metrics:fcfa2s_per_share:FY2025], growth of 26.9 then 14.3 percent computed here, on a share count unchanged at 21.19 million [ledger:shares_outstanding:FY2023] [ledger:shares_outstanding:Q2 2026]. FCFA2S compounded at 16.5 percent from 2018 to 2021 [hold:ten_year_history:longest_available:fcfa2s_cagr] and 25.4 percent from 2022 to 2025 [metrics:fcfa2s_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025].

2. The organic floor fails, and it always has. FX adjusted organic growth was 1 percent in Q2 and H1 2026 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026]. The 2015 to 2020 average was negative 0.8 percent and the longest window averages 0.5 percent [hold:ten_year_history:2015_2020:average_organic_growth_pct] [hold:ten_year_history:longest_available:average_organic_growth_pct]. This is an acquisition machine on a flat base. Every dollar of ten year return is bought, so the hold is a bet on the return earned on deployment, not on the installed base.

3. Return on deployment is the one number that decides it. At full reinvestment, per share growth equals the return earned [hold:reinvestment_grid:reinv 1.00 roic 0.10]. A third party ROIC of 10.97 percent against a stated 25 percent hurdle [ledger:unmapped:Hurdle rate IRR target:2024-2026] sits at the bottom of what the grid needs. Below 8 percent the decade returns what a bond returns. No operating group discloses a return on anything it has bought, so this number can only be read at the parent, after the fact, through FCFA2S per share.

4. The structure keeps producing allocators, which is why the verdict is Hold rather than Not Hold. Four successions since the end of 2023 were filled from inside: Perseus, Volaris, Harris and the parent [holdings:perseus:leadership] [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mike Dufton] [holdings:harris:leadership] [holdings:volaris:leadership:Mark Miller]. Five new units formed in four years, and Volaris alone produced Lumine, Vencora, Omegro and the current President [holdings:volaris:status_note]. That is the part of the moat that does not depend on any vertical or any one person.

Which holding companies carry the case

- Volaris carries the runway: 30 deals in 2025 [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], the incubator of the family. Strong, on structure.
- Harris and Topicus carry the moat: regulated systems of record, and the only clean organic read in the family, Topicus at 4 to 5 percent [hold:listed_subsidiaries:topicus:organic_growth_pct:FY2025]. Both Adequate, closest to strong.
- Jonas is the canary for AI pricing pressure: clubs, fitness and hospitality at 4.5 million of revenue per brand, computed from 840.2 across 185 brands [holdings:jonas:revenue] [holdings:jonas:business_count]. Adequate.
- Lumine is the early warning: FX adjusted organic of negative 9, 1 and negative 2 percent across FY2024, FY2025 and Q1 2026 [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2024] [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025] [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026]. Adequate.
- Perseus is the large deal arm, with a forty year seller note and no named CEO since the founder retired [holdings:perseus:largest_deal_price] [holdings:perseus:gaps]. Adequate.
- Vela is the least visible group; headcount is its only continuous series [holdings:vela:employees:2026-03]. Adequate, low confidence.
- Omegro is a test of incubation, not a source of value. Adequate.

None is weak. But none of the five private groups discloses revenue, cash flow, organic growth or a return, and they are most of the value: the two listed stakes are 9.21 to 9.95 percent of market cap on CAD figures [hold:look_through:share_of_csu_market_cap:topicus:reported_cad:single] [hold:look_through:share_of_csu_market_cap:lumine:share_of_csu_market_cap_reported_cad:high], so roughly 90 percent of what a holder owns is read only through parent totals.

Caveats that bound the call. No FX rate was sourced, so every look through share is CAD against CAD and the market cap conflict from the first report remains open [ledger:market_cap_usd:2026-09] [ledger:market_cap_cad:2026-09-08]. The Topicus stake is a January 2021 figure [holdings:topicus:csu_economic_interest_pct]. Most group level figures behind this analysis are third party, and four parent figures that intake attributed to Perseus and Vela had to be struck. The parent's FCFA2S record for 2015 to 2017 could not be found from company sources, so the decade long compounding claim rests on 2018 onward.

## The Question

The first report (analysis/constellation-software/report.md) asked whether Constellation Software was worth buying at CAD 2,827.94 on September 11, 2026, and returned Not Buy [ledger:share_price_cad:2026-09-11]. That question turns on price against a five year cash flow forecast. This report asks a different question: is the business worth holding for ten years, independent of price, and which of Constellation's eight holding companies carry that case. It reuses the first report's ledger.json and metrics.json figures where they apply and recomputes nothing.

Functional analysis here means the same eight part treatment for every holding company: what it does, what function it serves inside Constellation (capital deployer, cash generator, geographic arm, listed currency, or talent bench), what it owns, how it deploys capital, where its verticals sit on the AI exposure map, who runs it, and whether that function is durable over ten years.

## Is the Engine Durable

The private groups publish no financials, so three of the six tests below rest on counts and disclosure, not margins.

### 1. Reinvestment runway

At the first report's entry of USD 2,262.86 [hold:hold_grid_10yr.entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:entry_price_usd], ten years of 8 percent FCFA2S per share growth at a 25 times exit returns 8.74 percent a year [hold:hold_grid_10yr.entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:cagr 0.08 exit 25x], and 10 percent growth at 20 times returns 8.31 percent [hold:hold_grid_10yr.entry_usd_2262_86_first_report:cagr 0.10 exit 20x]. At the FX corrected entry of USD 2,080.29 both rise about a point [hold:hold_grid_10yr.entry_usd_2080_29_at_usdcad_1_3594:cagr 0.08 exit 25x] [hold:hold_grid_10yr.entry_usd_2080_29_at_usdcad_1_3594:cagr 0.10 exit 20x]. The decade needs 8 to 10 percent per share growth a year at a 20 to 25 times exit.

The identity is g equals reinvestment rate times return on deployed capital [hold:reinvestment_grid:formula]: 1.00 times FCFA2S at a 10 percent return gives 10 percent growth, at 8 percent it gives 8 percent [hold:reinvestment_grid:reinv 1.00 roic 0.10] [hold:reinvestment_grid:reinv 1.00 roic 0.08], below the stated 25 percent hurdle [ledger:unmapped:Hurdle rate IRR target:2024-2026]. The record: acquisitions ran 1.88, 0.63, 1.22 and 0.94 times FCFA2S across FY2022 to FY2025 [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2022] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2023] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2024] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025], while the reinvestment rate fell from 154.5 to 114.3 to 89.9 percent across 2023 to 2025 [ledger:unmapped:Reinvestment rate:2023, 2024, 2025]. H1 2026 cash consideration of 1,429 [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:H1 2026] against LTM FCFA2S of 2,030 [ledger:fcfa2s:LTM to Q2 2026] is already 0.70 of a year's flow in six months, computed here. Deployment is not the constraint; return is. Third party ROIC fell from 14.8 percent in 2025 to 10.97 percent at June 2026 [ledger:unmapped:ROIC:2025, June 2026, TTM 2026], and at full deployment and 11 percent the grid barely clears the band.

The runway sits with two groups. In 2025 Volaris closed 30 deals [holdings:volaris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], Harris 19 [holdings:harris:acquisitions_per_year:2025], Lumine 15 [stage1:lumine:https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/lumine-group-posts] and Jonas 10 [stage1:jonas:https://jonassoftware.com/2025-year-in-review], stage1 only for the last two: 74 deals, computed here, four groups unreported. Perseus deploys in size instead, 700 million for Optimal Blue and Empower [holdings:perseus:largest_deal_price]; Vela has closed 20 deals since inception [holdings:vela:acquisitions_per_year:since_inception]. The runway is two groups wide, one deep.

Verdict: Holds with conditions. Evidence weight: moderate.

### 2. Organic floor

Reported parent organic growth was negative 1, 5, 2 and 4 percent across FY2022 to FY2025 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2022] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2023] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2024] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2025]. FX adjusted it was 3 percent in FY2025, 2 in Q1 2026 and 1 in Q2 and H1 2026, against 4 percent in Q2 2025 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2025]. The longer record is worse: average organic growth was negative 0.8 percent for 2015 to 2020 and 0.5 percent across the longest window, with the 2.0 percent for 2020 to 2025 resting on two years only [hold:ten_year_history.2015_2020.average_organic_growth_pct] [hold:ten_year_history.longest_available.average_organic_growth_pct] [hold:ten_year_history.2020_2025.average_organic_growth_pct]. This engine has never had an organic floor above zero in real terms; 2023 to 2025 was the exception.

Above the line, only Topicus: 5 percent organic in FY2024, 4 in FY2025, 4 in Q2 2026 [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.organic_growth_pct:FY2024] [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.organic_growth_pct:FY2025] [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026], on a base roughly 70 percent maintenance [hold:listed_subsidiaries.topicus.maintenance_share_of_revenue]. Below it, Lumine ran 0 percent FX adjusted in FY2023, negative 9 in FY2024, 1 in FY2025, negative 2 in Q1 2026 [hold:listed_subsidiaries.lumine.organic_growth_pct:FY2023] [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2024] [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025] [holdings:lumine:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026], four years without a real gain.

Vela's organic figures were parent numbers misattributed at intake and were nulled [holdings:vela:organic_growth_pct]; Volaris, Harris, Jonas, Perseus and Omegro disclose none [holdings:volaris:organic_growth_commentary] [holdings:omegro:gaps], most of the company by revenue. The counterweight is the base: maintenance and recurring revenue rose from 71.2 percent of revenue in FY2023 to 76.6 percent in H1 2026 [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2023] [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026], and FY2025 maintenance organic growth was 6 percent [ledger:unmapped:FY2025 maintenance and recurring revenue organic growth]. The base is not shrinking in dollars, it is failing to outgrow prices.

Verdict: Fails. Evidence weight: moderate for the parent and the listed subsidiaries, none for the private groups.

## The Holding Companies

### Volaris

Volaris is Constellation's largest private operating group, a generalist covering 240 standalone software companies [holdings:volaris:business_count] across 40 verticals [holdings:volaris:vertical_count] in 60 countries [holdings:volaris:country_count], serving public agencies and mid market operators in transit, justice, public safety, government administration, insurance and digital banking [holdings:volaris:verticals]. Revenue is a gap, not a fact: the only three figures on file are third party and inconsistent, and two trace to a press release describing the Mexican airline Volaris, a different company with the same name, not the Constellation subsidiary [holdings:conflicts:volaris:revenue].
