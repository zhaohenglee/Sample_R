## Revenue: fast, but decelerating

Revenue went 8,407 in FY2023 to 10,066 in FY2024 to 11,623 in FY2025, USD millions [ledger:revenue:FY2023] [ledger:revenue:FY2024] [ledger:revenue:FY2025]. Growth stepped down each year: 27.0, 19.7, then 15.5 percent [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2023] [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2024] [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:FY2025]. The three year revenue CAGR to FY2025 is 20.6 percent [metrics:revenue_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025].

2026 reaccelerated on the reported line: 20.0 percent in Q1, 17.3 percent in Q2, 18.5 percent for H1 [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:Q1 2026] [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:Q2 2026] [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:H1 2026]. That reacceleration was bought, not earned. H1 2026 revenue of 6,516 against 5,498 [ledger:revenue:H1 2026] [ledger:revenue:H1 2025] came with organic growth of only 4 percent, and 1 percent after FX [ledger:organic_growth_pct:H1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026].

## Organic growth: the weak link

The organic series is the number that matters most and it is soft. FY2022 negative 1 percent, FY2023 5, FY2024 2, FY2025 4 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2022] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2023] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2024] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2025], with FY2025 at 3 percent after FX [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025]. Inside 2026 it deteriorated: 6 percent organic in Q1 to 3 percent in Q2 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:Q1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_pct:Q2 2026], and 2 percent to 1 percent FX adjusted [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026] [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2026]. Q2 2026 at 1 percent is below Q2 2025 at 4 percent [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2025]. Currency is doing about two points of work in the headline organic print.

This is not company specific. Topicus posted 5 percent organic in H1 2026 [peer:Topicus.com (TOI):H1 2026 organic growth]. Lumine printed 0 percent in Q1 2026 and negative 2 percent FX adjusted [peer:Lumine Group (LMN):Q1 2026 organic growth fx-adj]. Enghouse shrank, H1 2026 revenue down 5.8 percent [peer:Enghouse Systems (ENGH):H1 2026 revenue growth yoy]. Low single digit organic is the sector condition.

## Maintenance base: mixing higher

Maintenance and recurring revenue rose from 71.2 percent of revenue in FY2023 to 73.5 percent in FY2024 to 76.6 percent in H1 2026 [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2023] [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2024] [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026], or 4,994 dollars in H1 2026 [ledger:maintenance_recurring_revenue:H1 2026]. It grew 23.6 percent in FY2024 and 22.2 percent in Q1 2026 [metrics:maintenance_growth_yoy:FY2024] [metrics:maintenance_growth_yoy:Q1 2026]. The base is getting stickier as organic softens. That matters for the substitution question, since churn shows up in maintenance first and the maintenance share is rising. FY2025 and Q2 2025 maintenance dollars are missing from the ledger, so the share series has holes [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2025].

## Margins: cash is the honest line

Net margin is noisy and a poor read here: 6.7 percent FY2023, 7.3 percent FY2024, 4.4 percent FY2025 [metrics:net_margin:FY2023] [metrics:net_margin:FY2024] [metrics:net_margin:FY2025]. Acquired intangible amortisation moves it around.

Cash is cleaner. Operating cash flow margin improved from 21.2 percent in FY2023 to 23.5 percent in FY2025 [metrics:cfo_margin:FY2023] [metrics:cfo_margin:FY2025]. FCFA2S margin ran 12.9, 13.8, 14.6 and 14.5 percent across FY2022 to FY2025 [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:FY2022] [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:FY2023] [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:FY2024] [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:FY2025]. Flat to mildly up, no visible erosion. FCFA2S compounded at 25.4 percent over three years [metrics:fcfa2s_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025], faster than revenue, and LTM to Q2 2026 is 2,030 [ledger:fcfa2s:LTM to Q2 2026].

Adjusted EBITA is absent from the ledger for every period, so the company's preferred margin metric cannot be trended [metrics:adjusted_ebita_margin:FY2025]. Capex is absent for every period [metrics:capex_to_revenue:FY2025]. Both are gaps, not zeros.

## The acquisition engine

Acquisition cash deployed was 1,600 in FY2022, 732 in FY2023, 1,792 in FY2024, 1,579 in FY2025 [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2022] [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2023] [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2024] [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2025]. Against FCFA2S that is 1.88x, 0.63x, 1.22x, 0.94x [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2022] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2023] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2024] [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025]. The company's own reinvestment rate shows the same decline: 154.5 percent in 2023, 114.3 in 2024, 89.9 in 2025 [ledger:unmapped:Reinvestment rate:2023, 2024, 2025].

2026 is a step up. H1 2026 deployment was 1,700, more than all of FY2025 [ledger:acquisitions_cash_spent:H1 2026], and Q2 2026 alone spent 2.12x that quarter's FCFA2S [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:Q2 2026]. The engine is not short of targets.

Does it still compound? The inputs look healthy and the output is falling. ROIC went from 14.8 percent in 2025 to 10.97 percent at June 2026 [ledger:unmapped:ROIC:2025, June 2026, TTM 2026] against a stated 25 percent IRR hurdle [ledger:unmapped:Hurdle rate IRR target:2024-2026]. Nearly all growth is acquired: 18.5 percent reported H1 growth on 1 percent FX adjusted organic. That is a machine that still buys revenue at scale but no longer compounds much inside what it owns.

## Anomalies and what is one off

The Q2 2026 earnings jump is largely not operating. Net income attributable went from 56 to 274 [ledger:net_income_attributable:Q2 2025] [ledger:net_income_attributable:Q2 2026], lifting net margin from 2.0 to 8.2 percent [metrics:net_margin:Q2 2025] [metrics:net_margin:Q2 2026]. Two causes. The IRGA liability moved, a 13 revaluation charge against a 35 FX gain in H1 2026, on a balance falling from 1,234 to 1,186 [ledger:unmapped:IRGA liability balance and revaluation:H1 2026, Dec 2025]. And currency. The company's own net income growth excluding FX and IRGA was 8 percent [stage1:q2_2026.json:https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/11/3343207/0/en/constellation-software-inc-announces-results-for-the-second-quarter-ended-june-30-2026-and-declares-quarterly-dividend.html], not the reported multiple. So treat the H1 2026 net margin of 9.8 percent against 3.5 percent as mostly one off [metrics:net_margin:H1 2025] [metrics:net_margin:H1 2026].

Corroboration that it is non cash: cash conversion, FCFA2S over net income, collapsed from 3.93x in Q2 2025 to 1.26x in Q2 2026 [metrics:cash_conversion:Q2 2025] [metrics:cash_conversion:Q2 2026]. The durable part is FCFA2S, 220 to 345 [ledger:fcfa2s:Q2 2025] [ledger:fcfa2s:Q2 2026], with margin 7.7 to 10.3 percent [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:Q2 2025] [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:Q2 2026]. That improvement is real. The earnings headline is not.

Other flags. The trailing P/E of 93.7x uses FY2025 net income of 512, itself depressed [metrics:valuation:pe_trailing] [ledger:net_income_attributable:FY2025]; price to FCFA2S of 23.6x and a 4.2 percent FCFA2S yield are the usable multiples [metrics:valuation:price_to_fcfa2s_per_share] [metrics:valuation:fcfa2s_yield]. EV multiples could not be computed, the enterprise value entry being CAD with no USDCAD rate in the ledger [metrics:valuation:ev_to_revenue]. Market cap has a live conflict, 47,950 USD millions against a CAD 65,183 figure [ledger:market_cap_usd:2026-09]. FY2023 acquisition spend of 732 conflicts with 1,792, and FY2025 of 1,579 with 1,513, both unresolved.

## Top five risks

1. Organic growth going to zero. FX adjusted organic was 1 percent in Q2 2026 and for H1 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026]. At 23.6x FCFA2S the price assumes recovery; the grid gives a five year IRR of 1.1 percent at zero growth and a 25x exit [metrics:scenario_grid:growth 0.0 exit 25x].
2. Returns falling faster than spend rises. ROIC 14.8 to 10.97 percent while H1 2026 deployment hit 1,700. Deploying more at lower returns breaks the compounding math faster than deploying less.
3. FX reversal. Roughly two points of Q2 2026 organic growth and much of the net income jump are currency. Both unwind if the dollar strengthens.
4. Multiple compression. 23.6x now [metrics:valuation:current_multiple_price_to_fcfa2s] against a 15x exit gives a negative 4.1 percent IRR even at 5 percent growth [metrics:scenario_grid:growth 0.05 exit 15x]. Valuation risk exceeds operating risk at this price.
5. Leadership plus balance sheet. Net debt rose from 980 to 2,200 in one quarter under a President in post under a year. Capacity is still about one turn of LTM FCFA2S, but a faster pace, a falling ROIC and an untested allocator is the tail worth naming.
