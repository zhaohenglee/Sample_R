# AI Disruption Test of the Vertical Market Software Thesis

Lens: does an LLM agent replace vertical market software, and is Constellation's installed base already showing it? The user thesis is that it does not. Tested here, not assumed.

## Verdict up front

The thesis survives, but not for the reason usually given, and the data cannot yet falsify the opposite view. Nothing in the ledger looks like substitution of a system of record. What it does show is a continuing slowdown in organic growth that cannot be attributed to any single cause. The right framing is not "will Claude replace VMS" but "does AI shorten the economic life of each acquired business faster than the acquisition engine replaces it".

## What the numbers actually show

Organic growth has decelerated, and the FX adjusted series is the honest one. Reported organic growth ran 5 percent in FY2023 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2023], 2 percent in FY2024 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2024] and 4 percent in FY2025 [ledger:organic_growth_pct:FY2025]. Strip currency and FY2025 was 3 percent [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025], Q1 2026 was 2 percent [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q1 2026], Q2 2026 was 1 percent [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2026] and H1 2026 was 1 percent [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026], against 4 percent in Q2 2025 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:Q2 2025]. That is a genuine step down, not noise.

The maintenance base tells the opposite story. Maintenance and recurring revenue rose from 71.2 percent of total revenue in FY2023 [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2023] to 76.6 percent in H1 2026 [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026], and grew 22.2 percent year over year in Q1 2026 [metrics:maintenance_growth_yoy:Q1 2026] against 23.6 percent in FY2024 [metrics:maintenance_growth_yoy:FY2024]. FY2025 maintenance organic growth was 6 percent [ledger:unmapped:FY2025 maintenance and recurring revenue organic growth]. A base being displaced by agents does not rise as a share of revenue.

Cash conversion did not crack either. FCFA2S margin went from 12.9 percent in FY2022 [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:FY2022] to 14.5 percent in FY2025 [metrics:fcfa2s_margin:FY2025], with a three year FCFA2S CAGR of 25.4 percent [metrics:fcfa2s_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025] on 20.6 percent revenue CAGR [metrics:revenue_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025].

Two caveats matter. First, maintenance organic growth decelerated inside that picture: 6 percent constant currency in Q4 2025 [stage1:ai_and_leadership:https://expansestocks.substack.com/p/constellation-software-q4-2025], 4 percent FX neutral in Q1 2026 [stage1:q1_2026:https://seekingalpha.com/article/4905097-constellation-software-q1-2026-leaning-in-amidst-saaspocalypse], 2 percent in Q2 2026 [stage1:ai_and_leadership:https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9029244/constellation-software-inc-cnswf-q2-2026-earnings-call-highlights-ai-boosts-productivity-but-organic-growth-decelerates]. Those quarterly figures are stage1 evidence only and were not carried into the ledger. Second, no churn rate, seat count, retention or price versus volume split exists anywhere in metrics.json or ledger.json. The most diagnostic number for this question is absent, and everything below is inference around that hole.

## The moat, taken apart

**System of record and regulatory data.** Not eroded on any horizon visible here. Regulators do not accept a probabilistic layer as the authoritative copy of a permit file or a clinical chart. Horizon: beyond ten years, and only if regulators certify model outputs. Evidence that would show it: maintenance revenue falling in absolute dollars while total revenue holds, or maintenance share reversing below its FY2023 level of 71.2 percent [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:FY2023].

**Embedded workflow and integrations.** Partly eroded, horizon three to seven years. Agents are good at the integration and data movement work vendors bill as professional services, so the licence and services line gets hollowed before maintenance does. Evidence: total revenue growth holding up while the non maintenance remainder shrinks. Total revenue grew 18.5 percent in H1 2026 [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:H1 2026] while maintenance share rose, which is consistent with mix shifting away from services already.

**Switching cost and customer inertia.** Least eroded. Agents lower the cost of building a replacement, not of migrating a regulated customer off a running system. The CFO reviewed roughly 900 business units for AI driven attrition anomalies and disclosed no material finding [stage1:ai_and_leadership:https://expansestocks.substack.com/p/constellation-software-agm-2026]. Absence of disclosure is weak evidence, but it is the only direct read available.

**Pricing power.** Most exposed, shortest horizon, two to five years. Maintenance organic growth here is substantially price. If customers can point at an agent and argue the software is worth less, escalators compress. The decay from 6 percent to 2 percent across three quarters is the shape price compression takes, and also the shape ordinary budget pressure takes. The data cannot separate them. Evidence that would settle it: maintenance organic below 2 percent for four consecutive quarters while unit counts are flat.

**Small niches with no venture backed competitors.** Genuinely at risk, medium horizon. The old defence was that a niche of a few hundred customers could not support a funded entrant. If build cost collapses, a two person team can. The counter is that acquisition prices have not collapsed, with DerbySoft bought at 4.0 times revenue [stage1:capital_allocation:https://www.mbi-deepdives.com/csu2q26/], which is not what a market pricing in obsolescence looks like.

## Strongest case the thesis is wrong

FX adjusted organic growth of 1 percent in H1 2026 [ledger:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:H1 2026] is close to zero, and this is not company specific. Lumine ran 0 percent organic in Q1 2026 and negative 2 percent currency adjusted [peer:LMN:Q1 2026 organic growth fx-adj], and Enghouse revenue fell 5.8 percent year over year in H1 2026 [peer:ENGH:H1 2026 revenue growth yoy]. Three VMS names decelerating together is a sector signal, not an execution problem. Reported ROIC also fell from 14.8 percent in 2025 [stage1:capital_allocation:https://thepursuitofcompounding.substack.com/p/constellation-software-inc-fiscal] to 10.97 percent by June 2026 [stage1:capital_allocation:https://www.gurufocus.com/term/roic/CNSWF], on differing third party definitions against a 25 percent IRR hurdle [stage1:capital_allocation:https://www.morningstar.com/company-reports/1489931-constellation-software-hurdle-rate-discipline-remains-the-cash-compounders-north-star], so the picture is a machine paying yesterday's multiples for assets whose terminal value is being repriced down. The thesis would then be right about replacement and wrong about what matters, because value leaks through price and growth without a single customer switching.

## Strongest case the thesis is right

Maintenance share rising to 76.6 percent [metrics:maintenance_share_of_revenue:H1 2026] while the business grows revenue 18.5 percent [metrics:revenue_growth_yoy:H1 2026] and converts 23.5 percent of revenue to operating cash [metrics:cfo_margin:FY2025] is not the signature of substitution. Topicus held 5 percent organic growth in H1 2026 [peer:TOI:H1 2026 organic growth] and Descartes grew revenue 12 percent at a 45.2 percent EBITDA margin [peer:DSG:FY2026 revenue growth yoy] [peer:DSG:FY2026 adjusted_ebitda_margin], so the sector is dispersing, not collapsing. AI is showing up on the cost side first, with one unit resolving 82 percent of Tier 1 tickets without a human [stage1:ai_and_leadership:https://expansestocks.substack.com/p/constellation-software-agm-2026], which defends margin. And Constellation is a buyer, so cheaper VMS assets raise forward IRR even as they lower legacy value.

## My probability weighted view

These weights are my judgment, not data. Roughly 60 percent that the thesis holds in its literal form, meaning no material substitution of systems of record through 2031 and maintenance organic settling in the 2 to 4 percent band. Roughly 30 percent that it holds literally but fails economically, with maintenance organic at or below 1 percent as pricing power erodes and the acquisition engine has to run harder for the same FCFA2S. Roughly 10 percent that agents take real share in the thinner niches and maintenance dollars decline outright. The second branch is where the numbers are currently drifting and the one investors under weight.

## Market pricing versus the numbers

The stock is down about 53 percent from its all time high [stage1:market_data:https://seekingalpha.com/article/4856592-constellation-softwares-biggest-drawdown-in-20-years-is-forcing-a-rethink] at CAD 2,827.94 [ledger:share_price_cad:2026-09-11], a USD 47,950 million market cap [metrics:market_cap_usd:2026-09], 23.6 times LTM FCFA2S per share [metrics:price_to_fcfa2s_per_share:2026-09-11] for a 4.2 percent free cash flow yield [metrics:fcfa2s_yield:2026-09-11]. The scenario grid says that at a 25 times exit multiple, zero FCFA2S per share growth returns 1.1 percent annually [metrics:scenario_grid:growth 0.0, exit 25x], 5 percent growth returns 6.2 percent [metrics:scenario_grid:growth 0.05, exit 25x] and 10 percent growth returns 11.3 percent [metrics:scenario_grid:growth 0.10, exit 25x]. So the market is pricing mid to high single digit FCFA2S growth as fair value.

The numbers delivered 25.4 percent FCFA2S CAGR over three years [metrics:fcfa2s_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025] with acquisitions absorbing 0.94 times FCFA2S in FY2025 [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:FY2025] and 2.12 times in Q2 2026 [metrics:acquisitions_to_fcfa2s:Q2 2026]. The gap between roughly 6 percent implied and 25 percent delivered is the disruption discount. That is a large discount to carry on evidence this thin, which is the honest argument for the thesis and against the price. Note the trailing P/E of 93.7 times [metrics:pe_trailing:2026-09-11] is distorted by depressed FY2025 net income of 4.4 percent of revenue [metrics:net_margin:FY2025] versus 9.8 percent in H1 2026 [metrics:net_margin:H1 2026] and should not be used.

## What is missing

No churn, retention, seat count or price versus volume data exists in either file. Adjusted EBITA is absent for every period [metrics:adjusted_ebita_margin:FY2025], so no EV to EBITA comparison against history is possible. No data was retrieved for Roper, Tyler or Open Text [peer:ROP:status], so the large cap comparison is untested. Until maintenance organic growth is split into volume and price, neither side of this argument can be proven from the numbers.
