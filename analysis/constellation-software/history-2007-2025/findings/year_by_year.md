## The years

**FY2007.** Two years after listing, revenue of 243.0 grew 16 percent [hist:revenue:FY2007] [hist:revenue_growth_pct:FY2007], both figures third party since the company's own FY2007 release was not recoverable. That 16 percent is the number that mattered: it was below the 20 percent a year the IPO objective set for 2006 to 2010, which management called challenging but achievable while reporting 33 percent EBITDA growth [event:2007:ipo_growth_target_progress].

**FY2008.** Revenue rose 36.0 percent to 330.5 [hm:annual:revenue_growth_pct:FY2008] [hist:revenue:FY2008], again on third party data, while organic growth was about negative 10 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2008]. The negative 10 is the number that mattered, because it shows the headline was entirely acquired. The largest named deal was MAXIMUS Justice, Education and Asset Solutions on 30 September for USD 40 million, part of 94 million spent across roughly 17 deals [event:2008-09-30:acquisition] [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2008].

**FY2009.** Revenue of 438 is derived from the FY2010 release's statement that 2010 was 44 percent above 2009 [hist:revenue:FY2009], the only route to the figure. The number that mattered is ROIC of 24 percent [hist:roic_pct:FY2009], the first year the engine's return is on the record, earned while organic revenue fell 3 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2009]. No event for this year in the ledger.

**FY2010.** Revenue grew 44.1 percent to 631 [hm:annual:revenue_growth_pct:FY2010] [hist:revenue:FY2010] against organic growth of about negative 4 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2010]. That pairing is the year: the fastest reported growth in the whole 19 year record sat on a shrinking installed base, with ROIC of 25 percent doing the work [hist:roic_pct:FY2010]. A 2.00 CAD dividend was paid [hm:annual:dividends_per_share_total:FY2010]. No event for this year in the ledger.

**FY2011.** Net income of 157 [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2011] is the number that mattered, and it is the year's main conflict: a second reading of the same release gave 63, resolved in favour of 157 on two independent retrievals and a stated 75 million tax recovery, per the ledger's conflicts entry for this metric and year [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2011]. Three events land here. The board opened a review of strategic alternatives on 4 April, during which operating groups stopped entering new verticals and acquisition spending fell to under half the 2010 level [event:2011-04-04:strategic_review_2011]. Volaris was incorporated under its current name [event:2011:operating_group] and Friedman bought Markinson in December [event:2011-12:acquisition]. Organic growth and acquisition spend are both gap.

**FY2012.** Organic growth of positive 6 percent is the number that mattered [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2012], the first positive print in the series and still the second best of the 19 years. The strategic review closed without a sale and the board adopted a true quarterly dividend of USD 1.00 a share from Q1 [event:2012:strategic_review_outcome], which is why the 4.00 in the dividend column is marked derived rather than sourced for this year. Net income halved to 93 while adjusted net income rose to 172 [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2012] [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2012], the first clear gap between reported profit and the company's cash proxy.

**FY2013.** Acquisition spending of 558 is the number that mattered [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2013]: 269.6 percent of adjusted net income [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2013] and 435.8 negative after acquisitions and dividends [hm:annual:cash_after_acquisitions_and_dividends:FY2013], the most leveraged year in the record. The driver was Total Specific Solutions, agreed 16 December at about EUR 240 million and closed 31 December, then the largest deal in company history and funded with a one year USD 350 million term loan [event:2013-12-16:acquisition_above_100m]. Revenue of 1,211 is derived from the release's two segment lines, 832 public and 379 private, no consolidated total having been retrieved [hist:revenue:FY2013].

**FY2014.** Acquisition spend collapsed to 115 [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2014], 41.9 percent of adjusted net income [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2014], and that retrenchment is the year. Revenue still grew 37.8 percent [hm:annual:revenue_growth_pct:FY2014] on the full year effect of TSS, with organic growth at 4 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2014]. No event for this year in the ledger.

**FY2015.** Revenue growth of 10.1 percent is the number that mattered [hm:annual:revenue_growth_pct:FY2015], the slowest of the 19 years, with organic growth back at negative 3 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2015]. Against that, adjusted EBITA margin reached 24.3 percent [hm:annual:adjusted_ebita_margin_pct:FY2015], the highest so far, on 248.8 of acquisitions [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2015]. The acquisition count is gap: the only figure found counted publicly announced major deals, not the year's total. No event for this year in the ledger.

**FY2016.** Organic growth of exactly 0 percent is the year [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2016], and it is also a resolved conflict: three sources gave 0, negative 1 and positive 1, settled on the FY2016 MD&A [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2016]. CFO of 491 [hist:cash_from_operations:FY2016] and adjusted net income of 395.0 against reported net income of 206.8 [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2016] [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2016] show the acquired intangible amortisation wedge at close to double. Adjusted EBITA is gap. No event for this year in the ledger.

**FY2017.** Adjusted EBITA margin of 25.1 percent is the number that mattered [hm:annual:adjusted_ebita_margin_pct:FY2017], the peak of the entire series and the last good reading before the metric disappears from the record. Revenue of 2,479 is third party and approximate [hist:revenue:FY2017], and acquisition spend is gap, so the year's deployment cannot be read at all. No event for this year in the ledger.

**FY2018.** The USD 20.00 special dividend is the year [hist:dividends_per_share_special:FY2018], declared with the FY2018 results on 14 February 2019, management saying it had capital in excess of its needs [event:2019-02-14:special_dividend]. Total distributions of 24.00 a share [hm:annual:dividends_per_share_total:FY2018] took cash after acquisitions and dividends to negative 552.6 [hm:annual:cash_after_acquisitions_and_dividends:FY2018]. It is also the first FCFA2S year at 559 [hist:fcfa2s:FY2018] and the year Mark Leonard wrote his last full annual letter [event:2018:president_letter_policy_change]. No special dividend appears in any other year.

**FY2019.** Acquisition spend of 688 is the number that mattered [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2019], 116.6 percent of FCFA2S [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2019], the promised follow through on the excess capital argument. Organic growth turned negative again at 1 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2019]. FCFA2S became a reported metric with the Q3 release, replacing adjusted net income as the company's cash language [event:2019-10-31:fcfa2s_introduced]. The 688 itself is a resolved conflict against a 549 figure from the AIF [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2019], and the acquisition count is gap for the same reason as FY2015.

**FY2020.** FCFA2S of 989 against acquisitions of only 179 is the year [hist:fcfa2s:FY2020] [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2020], a reinvestment rate of 18.1 percent [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2020] and 725.2 left after acquisitions and dividends [hm:annual:cash_after_acquisitions_and_dividends:FY2020]. COVID pushed organic growth to negative 3 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2020] while CFO crossed a billion for the first time at 1,186 [hist:cash_from_operations:FY2020]. No event for this year in the ledger, but the unspent 989 is the direct cause of the following year's policy change.

**FY2021.** Organic growth of 7 percent is the number that mattered [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2021], the best in the 19 year record and never repeated. Topicus.com was spun out on 5 January [event:2021-01-05:spin_out], and Leonard's February letter announced Constellation would accept a lower hurdle rate on larger acquisitions to deploy the 2020 excess, noting brokers had included it in only about 16 percent of large VMS processes [event:2021-02:hurdle_rate_change]. Spending followed immediately: 1,337, or 151.4 percent of FCFA2S [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2021] [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2021].

**FY2022.** Acquisitions of 1,600 at 187.6 percent of FCFA2S is the year [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2022] [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2022], the highest reinvestment rate of the FCFA2S era, though the 1,600 is third party. Harris agreed to buy Allscripts Hospitals and Large Physician Practices on 2 March for up to USD 700 million, a segment with 2021 gross revenue of USD 928 million, completing 2 May [event:2022-03-02:acquisition_above_100m]. Organic growth was negative 1 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2022] and CFO was 1,297, two million below 2021 [hist:cash_from_operations:FY2022].

**FY2023.** Organic growth of 5 percent is the number that mattered [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2023], the best since 2021, in a year with three events. Lumine Group was spun out on 22 February after completing the WideOrbit purchase [event:2023-02-22:spin_out], Perseus completed Black Knight's Empower and Optimal Blue for about USD 700 million on 15 September as a forced ICE divestiture [event:2023-09-15:acquisition_above_100m], and August brought a warrant dividend and a debenture rights offering, both non cash and not a cash special [event:2023-08:non_cash_special_distribution]. Reported acquisition cash of 732 is low against those deals because much of the Black Knight price was a promissory note [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2023].

**FY2024.** Acquisition spend of 1,792 is the number that mattered [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2024], the largest single year of deployment in the record at 121.7 percent of FCFA2S [hm:annual:acquisitions_to_cash_metric_pct:FY2024]. Organic growth fell to 2 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2024] even as maintenance and recurring revenue reached 7,396 [hist:maintenance_recurring_revenue:FY2024]. The only ledger event is Perseus completing Auto-IT in March at an undisclosed price [event:2024-03:acquisition_undisclosed_price].

**FY2025.** Net income of 512 against CFO of 2,732 is the year [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2025] [hist:cash_from_operations:FY2025], a cash conversion ratio of 5.34 [hm:annual:cash_conversion_ratio:FY2025] and a net margin of 4.4 percent [hm:annual:net_margin_pct:FY2025], the lowest since FY2008. Reported profit fell while cash rose, which is what amortisation of acquired intangibles does at this scale. Mark Leonard resigned as President on 25 September citing health, with Mark Miller appointed the same day and the shares down 5.5 percent to C$3,910.71 [event:2025-09-25:leadership_change]. Constellation also bought TECVIA in November at an undisclosed price [event:2025-11:acquisition_undisclosed_price].

## Gaps in this table

Adjusted EBITA has no value after FY2018 [hm:annual:adjusted_ebita_margin_pct:FY2019]; two third party FY2022 and FY2023 estimates were rejected as margin assumptions applied to revenue, and they contradicted each other. Acquisition count exists for only 5 of 19 years: the FY2015 and FY2019 figures of 3 were withdrawn to gap because they counted publicly announced major deals rather than totals, and the FY2021 count of 85 is third party and stated as a minimum. Acquisition spend is gap for FY2007, FY2009 through FY2012 and FY2017, so the first era's deployment is unreadable apart from FY2008. CFO is gap for five years, all before FY2013. The FY2012, FY2016, FY2017 and FY2021 dividends are derived, carried at the quarterly USD 1.00 rate that is company sourced either side of them with no change announced. FY2010 and FY2011 are CAD while everything from FY2012 is USD, so the dividend column is not one currency. Year end share price is gap for all 19 years, confirmed after dedicated search passes in every intake window.
