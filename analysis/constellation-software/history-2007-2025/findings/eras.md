# Four eras, 2006 to 2025

The share count is a labelled assumption of 21.19 million [hist:shares_outstanding:FY2006], carried forward from FY2006 to FY2022 from two third party statements that it is unchanged since the IPO; only FY2025 is company sourced [hist:shares_outstanding:FY2025]. See the ledger conflicts record. Because the denominator never moves, every per share CAGR here equals its total CAGR exactly [hm:eras:revenue_per_share_cagr_pct:FY2007 to FY2025], an artefact of that assumption rather than a finding, and not repeated below.

## Era 1: Listing and proving the model, 2006 to 2011

**The aim.** Constellation listed on the TSX on 17 May 2006 at C$17.00, largely as a TD Capital liquidity event [event:2006-05-17:ipo], and attached a public test: Net Revenues per share and Adjusted EBITDA per share growing above 20 percent a year from 2006 to 2010 [event:2006:ipo_growth_target]. Jonas dated from 2003 [stage1:years_2007_2011.json:https://jonassoftware.com/20-years-of-jonas] and Friedman from 1999 [stage1:years_2007_2011.json:https://mergr.com/transaction/constellation-software-acquires-friedman], and Volaris was incorporated under that name in 2011 out of Trapeze, the 1995 first acquisition [event:2011:operating_group].

**The numbers.** Revenue went 243.02, a third party figure, to 773 USD millions [hist:revenue:FY2007] [hist:revenue:FY2011], a 33.55 percent CAGR over the four compounding years the ledger allows and the fastest of the four eras [hm:eras:revenue_cagr_pct:FY2007 to FY2011]. ROIC was 24 percent in FY2009 and 25 in FY2010 [hist:roic_pct:FY2009] [hist:roic_pct:FY2010], the only two such figures in the entire record.

None of the growth was organic: about negative 10 percent in FY2008, negative 3 in FY2009, about negative 4 in FY2010 [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2008] [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2009] [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2010], an era average of negative 5.67 percent on three of five years [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2007 to FY2011]. Deployment is close to unrecorded: only FY2008 has a spend, about 94 USD millions across the Maximus assets and sixteen other businesses [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2008] [hist:acquisition_count:FY2008], so the era total is meaningless [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2007 to FY2011]. Cash is thinner still, adjusted net income of 62 in FY2009 and 140 in FY2011 [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2009] [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2011], so no era cash CAGR exists [hm:eras:cash_metric_cagr_pct:FY2007 to FY2011].

**Defining decision.** On 4 April 2011 the board put the company up for sale, internally "The Process"; during the nine month review the groups stopped entering new verticals and 2011 spend fell below half of 2010's [event:2011-04-04:strategic_review_2011].

**Verdict: not assessable against its own test.** FY2006 revenue and Adjusted EBITDA are absent and the share count is an assumption, so both per share CAGRs and the pass or fail are null [hm:ipo_targets:met_target:FY2006 to FY2010]. The letters are the only evidence. Management reported 16 percent net revenue growth and 33 percent EBITDA growth for 2007, after 27 and 31 percent in 2006, calling the objective "challenging, but achievable" [event:2007:ipo_growth_target_progress] [hist:revenue_growth_pct:FY2007]; FY2010 then grew 44 percent [hist:revenue_growth_pct:FY2010]. Third party commentary claims the target was exceeded, with no primary confirmation found [stage1:years_2007_2011.json:https://www.csisoftware.com/docs/default-source/investor-relations/presidents-letter/shareholder-letter---2010.pdf]. It delivered on reported growth and returns; against its stated yardstick it is unverified.

## Era 2: Scaling and returning capital, 2012 to 2016

**The aim.** Stay independent and keep compounding. The review ended without a sale and the board adopted a true USD 1.00 quarterly dividend from Q1 2012, after about 2 per share for 2011 [event:2012:strategic_review_outcome] [hist:dividends_per_share_regular:FY2011] [hist:dividends_per_share_regular:FY2012].

**The numbers.** Revenue went 891.2 to 2,125.1 USD millions, 24.26 percent a year [hist:revenue:FY2012] [hist:revenue:FY2016] [hm:eras:revenue_cagr_pct:FY2012 to FY2016]; adjusted net income, the operative cash proxy then, went 172 to 395.0, 23.10 percent a year on that basis [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2012] [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2016] [hm:eras:cash_metric_cagr_pct:FY2012 to FY2016], the only clean same basis pair in the record. Net income did not follow: it sat flat at 93 in both FY2012 and FY2013 [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2012] [hist:net_income_attributable:FY2013] against FY2013 cash conversion of 2.37 times [hm:annual:cash_conversion_ratio:FY2013], the first clear sign that acquired intangible amortisation had decoupled earnings from cash.

Organic averaged 2.2 percent across all five years [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2012 to FY2016], running 6, 4, 4, negative 3 and 0 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2012] [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2015] [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2016]. The slowdown was largely currency, about positive 3 percent FX adjusted in FY2015 and positive 1 in FY2016 [hist:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2015] [hist:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2016], the FY2016 reported figure carrying an unresolved three way conflict. Deployment was lumpy, 558 USD millions on 30 deals in FY2013 then 115 on 23 in FY2014 [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2013] [hist:acquisition_count:FY2013] [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2014], totalling 1,099.9 and averaging 96.62 percent of cash from operations, the highest reinvestment intensity of any era [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2012 to FY2016] [hm:eras:avg_acquisitions_to_cfo_pct:FY2012 to FY2016]. Dividends totalled 20.00 per share [hm:eras:total_dividends_per_share:FY2012 to FY2016].

**Defining decision.** Not selling, then fixing the dividend rather than raising it. And Total Specific Solutions, agreed 16 December 2013 for about EUR 240 million, the largest deal to that date, funded with a one year USD 350 million term loan [event:2013-12-16:acquisition_above_100m], establishing both the appetite for size and the willingness to use debt.

**Verdict: delivered.** Revenue and the cash proxy both compounded above 23 percent [hm:eras:revenue_cagr_pct:FY2012 to FY2016] [hm:eras:cash_metric_cagr_pct:FY2012 to FY2016] and the company stayed independent. The blemish is organic growth at 2.2 percent, negative in one year [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2012 to FY2016].

## Era 3: Larger deals and looser hurdles, 2017 to 2021

**The aim.** Deploy a cash pile growing faster than the pipeline could absorb, and when that failed, hand the excess back once and change the rules rather than repeat it.

**The numbers.** Revenue went 2,479, a third party approximation, to 5,106, a 19.80 percent CAGR and the slowest era [hist:revenue:FY2017] [hist:revenue:FY2021] [hm:eras:revenue_cagr_pct:FY2017 to FY2021]. Cash from operations went 528 to 1,300, 25.26 percent a year [hist:cash_from_operations:FY2017] [hist:cash_from_operations:FY2021] [hm:eras:cfo_cagr_pct:FY2017 to FY2021]. No cash metric CAGR exists here and none can: the era opens on adjusted net income of 463 and closes on FCFA2S of 883 [hist:adjusted_net_income:FY2017] [hist:fcfa2s:FY2021] [hm:eras:cash_metric_cagr_pct:FY2017 to FY2021], FCFA2S having been adopted only with Q3 2019 results [event:2019-10-31:fcfa2s_introduced]. The metrics file prints a per share figure of 17.52 percent, but it chains the two and must not be used [hm:eras:cash_metric_per_share_cagr_pct:FY2017 to FY2021].

Organic averaged 1.8 percent [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2017 to FY2021], running 3, 3, negative 1, negative 3 and 7 percent [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2017] [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2021]. COVID FY2020 was negative 3 percent reported and after FX, so currency was not the excuse [hist:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2020]. Spend ran 603, 688, 179 and 1,337 USD millions across FY2018 to FY2021, two carrying unresolved basis conflicts [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2018] [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2020] [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2021], averaging 76.55 percent of cash from operations [hm:eras:avg_acquisitions_to_cfo_pct:FY2017 to FY2021]. FY2020 is the extreme, FCFA2S of 989 against acquisitions of 179, leaving 810 USD millions unspent [hist:fcfa2s:FY2020] [hm:annual:cash_after_acquisitions:FY2020].

**Defining decisions.** Three, all about cash the machine could not eat. The USD 20.00 special dividend of 14 February 2019, declared on the stated view that capital exceeded needs [event:2019-02-14:special_dividend] [hist:dividends_per_share_special:FY2018]; FY2018 dividends therefore total 24.00 per share and the year ends 552.56 USD millions in deficit [hm:annual:dividends_per_share_total:FY2018] [hm:annual:cash_after_acquisitions_and_dividends:FY2018]. It was never repeated. Second, the February 2021 letter lowering the hurdle on large deals, reasoning that brokers had included Constellation in only about 16 percent of large VMS sale processes [event:2021-02:hurdle_rate_change]. Third, the Topicus spin out of 5 January 2021 [event:2021-01-05:spin_out]. Leonard's 2018 letter was his last as a matter of course [event:2018:president_letter_policy_change], which is why the stated aims of eras 3 and 4 are thinner.

**Verdict: mixed.** Operating cash compounded at 25.26 percent [hm:eras:cfo_cagr_pct:FY2017 to FY2021] and the company admitted it could not deploy the result. But growing by buying produced the slowest revenue CAGR of the four, and the fix was to lower the price of admission.

## Era 4: Big deals and succession, 2022 to 2025

**The aim.** Spend at the new large deal hurdle, spin out what can stand alone, hand the company over.

**The numbers.** Revenue went 6,622 to 11,623 USD millions, 20.63 percent a year [hist:revenue:FY2022] [hist:revenue:FY2025] [hm:eras:revenue_cagr_pct:FY2022 to FY2025]. FCFA2S went 853 to 1,683, 25.42 percent a year on a clean single basis, the only usable cash CAGR besides era 2's [hist:fcfa2s:FY2022] [hist:fcfa2s:FY2025] [hm:eras:cash_metric_cagr_pct:FY2022 to FY2025] [metrics:fcfa2s_cagr_3yr:FY2022-FY2025], with cash from operations compounding at 28.19 percent [hm:eras:cfo_cagr_pct:FY2022 to FY2025]. Cash grew faster than revenue. Net income is not a usable read: net margin was 7.73 percent in FY2022 and 4.41 in FY2025, against FY2025 cash conversion of 5.34 times [hm:annual:net_margin_pct:FY2022] [hm:annual:net_margin_pct:FY2025] [hm:annual:cash_conversion_ratio:FY2025].

Organic averaged 2.5 percent on full coverage, the best era average and still barely above nothing: negative 1, 5, 2 and 4 percent [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2022 to FY2025] [hist:organic_growth_pct:FY2024], with FY2025 at 3 percent after FX [hist:organic_growth_fx_adj_pct:FY2025]. Deployment is the signature: spend ran 1,600, 732, 1,792 and 1,579 USD millions, the first third party [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2022] [hist:acquisitions_cash_spent:FY2024], totalling 5,703 [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2022 to FY2025]. That exceeds the previous three eras combined, 94 plus 1,099.9 plus 2,807 equals 4,000.9, computed here [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2007 to FY2011] [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2012 to FY2016] [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2017 to FY2021]. Reinvestment against the cash metric ran 187.57, 63.10, 121.74 and 93.82 percent, drifting down after the 2022 surge [hm:reinvestment:reinvestment_rate_pct:FY2022] [hm:reinvestment:reinvestment_rate_pct:FY2025].

**Defining decisions.** Two deals of unprecedented size. Allscripts Hospitals and Large Physician Practices for up to USD 700 million in March 2022, on a segment with 2021 revenue of USD 928 million [event:2022-03-02:acquisition_above_100m], and Black Knight's Empower and Optimal Blue for about USD 700 million in September 2023 [event:2023-09-15:acquisition_above_100m]. The Lumine spin out with WideOrbit in February 2023 [event:2023-02-22:spin_out], and a non cash warrant dividend in August 2023 rather than another cash special [event:2023-08:non_cash_special_distribution]. Then succession: Leonard resigned on 25 September 2025 citing health, Mark Miller was appointed, and the shares fell 5.5 percent, about C$4.8 billion of market value [event:2025-09-25:leadership_change].

**Verdict: delivered on its stated aim, failed the underlying one.** The 2021 large deal ambition [event:2021-02:hurdle_rate_change] was met, twice, and cash per share compounded at 25.42 percent [hm:eras:cash_metric_per_share_cagr_pct:FY2022 to FY2025]. But the prior report finds ROIC falling from 14.8 percent in 2025 to 10.97 percent at June 2026 against a stated 25 percent IRR hurdle [ledger:unmapped:ROIC:2025, June 2026, TTM 2026] [ledger:unmapped:Hurdle rate IRR target:2024-2026]. Bigger deals bought the growth; they did not restore the return.

## What stayed constant across all four

**The share count and the dividend.** Flat at 21.19 million throughout, confirmed only at the end [hist:shares_outstanding:FY2025]: no dilution, no buyback, no equity issued for deals. The dividend has been USD 1.00 per quarter from Q1 2012 to FY2025, never raised [hist:dividends_per_share_regular:FY2013] [hist:dividends_per_share_regular:FY2025], four years of it derived from the unchanged rate, plus one special ever: 80.00 per share over sixteen of nineteen years [hm:eras:total_dividends_per_share:FY2007 to FY2025]. Retention policy, not income policy.

**Organic growth never carried the company.** The full window average is 0.76 percent across seventeen of nineteen years [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2007 to FY2025], and no era averaged above 2.5 percent [hm:eras:avg_organic_growth_pct:FY2022 to FY2025]. Whatever this engine is, it is not an organic one.

**Acquisitions consumed most internal cash.** They averaged 83.05 percent of cash from operations over the twelve years where both exist, 9,703.9 USD millions recorded in thirteen of nineteen [hm:eras:avg_acquisitions_to_cfo_pct:FY2007 to FY2025] [hm:eras:total_acquisitions_spent:FY2007 to FY2025]. That understates the truth: six years carry no spend figure.

**Revenue compounded without a down year.** Era CAGRs of 33.55, 24.26, 19.80 and 20.63 percent give 23.97 percent for the full window [hm:eras:revenue_cagr_pct:FY2007 to FY2025], the weakest year still 10.13 percent in FY2015 [hm:annual:revenue_growth_pct:FY2015]. Revenue per share went 11.47 to 548.51 [hm:annual:per_share_revenue:FY2007] [hm:annual:per_share_revenue:FY2025].

**What did not stay constant: the yardstick.** The cash metric changed from adjusted net income to FCFA2S, so no full window cash CAGR exists [hm:eras:cash_metric_cagr_pct:FY2007 to FY2025] and era 3's is null by design. Disclosure narrowed: the ledger's last adjusted EBITA figure is FY2018 [hist:adjusted_ebita:FY2018] and its last ROIC figure FY2010 [hist:roic_pct:FY2010]. Returns fell while spending rose. Year end share prices are gap for every fiscal year, so no era return exists [hm:eras:share_price_cagr_cad_pct:FY2007 to FY2025]; only a price CAGR of 28.618 percent from the C$17.00 IPO price to the C$2,827.94 close on 11 September 2026, no dividends reinvested [hm:tsr:ipo_to_2026_price_cagr_cad_pct:2006-05-17 to 2026-09-11], and third party aggregates of 19.63 percent a year over ten years and 3,597.83 percent over fifteen [hist:aggregate_returns:annualized_return_10yr_pct] [hist:aggregate_returns:total_return_15yr_pct]. Every return figure here is third party or rests on the IPO price.
