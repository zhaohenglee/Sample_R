American frontier. When the pioneers and the settlers were moving west to 
stake out and take over land and start farming it and so on, these wagons

took to defend against the Indians was to circle the wagons, which means, 
you put everything, your crown jewels in the center of the circle, and then you 
fight, and you try to protect the center. It's a similar concept in investing with 
the Nifty 50 with Walmart, you really need to not touch it. It only works if you 
don't touch it.
