## The canonical combined top 100: which columns, how merged, what came out

### Takeaway
The combined list is saved with 100 rows: 76 A shares and 24 Hong Kong or US listings, with a rank 100 cutoff of 13.941 percent a year (rank 101 is China Resources Jiangzhong A share at 13.938 percent, so the cutoff is a near tie). Ranks 1 to 34 are Tencent, Fudan Microelectronics and Grand Pharma on top, then Gree, Zangge, Shengyi, Moutai, Sanhua, NHU, BYD, NetEase and so on; nine of the 34 are Hong Kong or US listings.

### Inferences
- The top 34 is a mix of true long run compounders (Tencent, Gree, Moutai, NetEase, Pien Tze Huang, Gujing, Hengrui, Sanhua) and names whose 20 year return came from reverse mergers into ST shells, asset injections or commodity cycles (Zangge, AVIC Shenyang, Sanan, Chifeng Gold, Hengli, Glarun, Northern Rare Earth); the sections below separate them.
- Rank 100 is a tie within 0.01 percentage point of ranks 98 to 102, so anything cited as "top 100" at the cutoff is fragile to the data corrections.

### Gaps
- Chinese names for Hong Kong and US rows beyond the HKEX short name were not checked against company filings; the truncated HKEX entry for Brilliance China (rank 70) is written as gap in the file.
- Dual listing detection relies on the screen's own `a_share_ticker` field (Yahoo name search with hand removal of false positives); an A+H pair the screen missed would not be merged. I checked the top 100 names by eye and found no other pair.

## Core business and ROE record of ranks 1 to 34

### Takeaway
Eleven of the 34 had ROE at or above 15 percent in at least 8 of the 10 years 2016 to 2025 (Moutai, Gree, Tencent and Sanhua in all 10; NetEase, Gujing and Pien Tze Huang in 9; Zangge, NHU, Changchun High-Tech and CSPC in 8); four never did (IEIT, Xuguang, Zhongtian, Huagong). The highest 2016 to 2025 averages are Moutai (31.9), Gree (26.4), Tencent (25.3), NetEase (24.0) and Pien Tze Huang and Sino Biopharm (21.9 each). Several high looking figures are distorted by one-offs, near-zero equity or restated reverse merger history (Zangge, Grand Pharma, Chifeng Gold, Sino Biopharm), flagged below.

### Cited Findings
**Definitions and sources**
- A shares (25 companies): weighted average ROE (加权平均净资产收益率) as first reported, taken from the 31 December column of the Sina Finance financial guideline page of each year (same rule as roe_prefetch.csv). Sina shows "--" for FY2016 for every company I fetched, so 2016 uses Eastmoney's weighted ROE (ROEJQ), marked `*` in the series; where Sina and Eastmoney differ by more than 0.05 the Eastmoney value is stored as the alternative — [Sina Shengyi FY2016 page](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/go.php/vFD_FinancialGuideLine/stockid/600183/ctrl/2016/displaytype/4.phtml) shows "--"; [Eastmoney annual indicators](https://datacenter.eastmoney.com/securities/api/data/v1/get?reportName=RPT_F10_FINANCE_MAINFINADATA&columns=ALL&filter=%28SECUCODE="600183.SH"%29%28REPORT_TYPE="年报"%29)
- Hong Kong names (Fudan Micro, Grand Pharma, Sino Biopharm, Jilin Changlong, Weichai, CSPC; also Tencent and BYD from the prefetch): net profit attributable divided by the average of opening and closing parent equity (Eastmoney HK `ROE_AVG`). I recomputed it from Eastmoney's profit and equity fields for the six new names: it matches to within 0.1 point in most years; differences larger than 1 point are Grand Pharma 2008 to 2009 (near zero equity), Sino Biopharm 2019 (reported 8.97, recomputed 12.51), CSPC 2011 (4.01 vs 10.19) and CSPC 2018 (24.45 vs 22.13). ## Was the ROE sustainable, and what else drove the return

### Takeaway
Verdicts: **yes** for 7 (Tencent, Gree, Moutai, Sanhua, NetEase, Gujing, Pien Tze Huang); **partly** for 16 (Fudan Micro, Grand Pharma, Zangge, Shengyi, NHU, BYD, AVIC Shenyang, Jushi, Sino Biopharm, Hengrui, Changchun High-Tech, Hundsun, Jilin Changlong, Weichai, Sieyuan, CSPC); **no** for 11 (IEIT, Sanan, Northern Rare Earth, Hengtong, Xuguang, Zhongtian, Chifeng Gold, Glarun, Huagong, Hengli, Chuantou). Where ROE was not sustained the return came from five routes: profit growth from a near-zero base (IEIT, Huagong, Zhongtian, Hengtong), reverse merger or asset injection into an ST shell (Zangge, AVIC Shenyang, Sanan, Hengli, Glarun, Chifeng), commodity or price cycles (Northern Rare Earth, Chifeng, Zangge, Jushi), one-off gains (Hundsun, Sino Biopharm) and re-rating (Fudan, Xuguang).

### Inferences
- Pricing power or a licensed or monopoly asset explains the "yes" group (Moutai pricing, Pien Tze Huang formula, Gree brand and channel, Tencent and NetEase platform and IP, Sanhua embedded components); brand and licences also explain the high years of the "partly" pharma names, but policy price cuts (volume-based procurement, reimbursement list pricing) ended their high ROE (Hengrui, Changchun High-Tech, CSPC, Sino Biopharm, Grand Pharma).
- At least eleven of the 34 started the window as ST shells, with tiny profits or negative equity (Zangge, AVIC Shenyang, Sanan, Chifeng, Hengli, Glarun, Grand Pharma) or grew from a near-zero profit base at low ROE (Hengtong, Zhongtian, IEIT, Huagong), so their 20 year CAGR is not evidence of a high return business.
