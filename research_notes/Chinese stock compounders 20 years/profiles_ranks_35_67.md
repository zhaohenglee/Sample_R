## Profiles, ROE record and verdicts

### Takeaway
Verdict counts below are from the rule: yes means ROE at or above 15% in at least 8 of the 10 years 2016 to 2025 or a documented durable franchise with a minimum above 12%; no means commodity cycle, one off boom, thin margins or shell/theme driven; partly otherwise. Counts are printed at the end of this section. Companies that did not sustain ROE were driven by commodity prices (gold, silver, refrigerants, cement, PTA, freight), asset injections and reverse takeovers (Hengyi, Shandong Gold, Shanjin, Hongfa, Topchoice, Zhongding, Hong Kong shells Art Group and Beijing Enterprises Water), disposal gains (Darentang), or theme rerating (Etern, Vital Deeptech, Unigroup).
