## 4. Debt note, ABL, and the 2025 refinancing

### Takeaway
One funded instrument: 700.0 of 10.625% senior secured notes due 15 Jan 2031, fair value 717.5. A 125.0 ABL to June 2030, undrawn, availability 92.2. The ABL springs to maturity if U.S. pension contributions above 100.0 fall due, unless liquidity above 130.0 and other conditions are met. The only financial covenant is a springing fixed charge coverage ratio, tested when availability is low.

### Cited Findings
- 2031 notes: face 700.0, carrying 687.2 (unamortized costs 12.8), fair value 717.5 (Level 2); interest 15 Jan and 15 Jul from 15 Jan 2026; senior secured, guaranteed by Unisys Holding Corporation, Unisys AP Investment Company I and Unisys NPL, Inc.; liens on substantially all assets, with ABL collateral liens subordinated to ABL lenders; structurally subordinated to non-guarantor liabilities "including ... pension liabilities" — [10-K Note 13 pp.76-78](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- Call terms: callable from 15 Jan 2028 at declining premiums to par from 15 Jan 2030; make-whole before 2028; up to 40% equity clawback at 110.625%; up to 10% a year at 103% before 2028; change of control put at 101% — [10-K Note 13 p.78](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- Indenture covenants are incurrence style (debt, restricted payments, liens, investments, asset sales, affiliate deals); no maintenance ratio is described — [10-K Note 13 p.78](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- 2031 notes interest expense 2025: coupon 38.0 plus 1.3 amortization = 39.3. 2027 notes: 16.9 in 2025 (34.5 in 2024) — [10-K Note 13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- Maturities (excluding finance leases): 2026 5.4, 2027 4.3, 2028 2.4, 2029 1.2, 2030 nil, thereafter 700.0; principal total 713.3 — [10-K Note 13 p.77](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- ABL: "up to an aggregate amount of $125.0 million (with a limit on letters of credit of $40.0 million), with an uncommitted accordion feature ... up to $155.0 million"; maturity extended "from October 2027 to June 2030"; "no borrowings and $13.5 million of letters of credit outstanding. Availability under the credit facility was $92.2 million, net of letters of credit issued"; borrowing base "calculated by reference to the company's receivables"; agent Bank of America, N.A.; cross default threshold 50.0 — [10-K Note 13 p.79](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- Springing maturity, verbatim: "The Amended and Restated ABL Credit Facility is subject to a springing maturity, under which the Amended and Restated ABL Credit Facility will immediately mature 91 days prior to any date on which contributions to pension funds in the United States in an amount in excess of $100.0 million are required to be paid unless the company is able to meet certain conditions, including that the company has the liquidity (as defined in the Amended and Restated ABL Credit Facility) to cash settle the amount of such pension payments, as applicable, no default or event of default has occurred under the Amended and Restated ABL Credit Facility, the company's liquidity is above $130.0 million and the company is in compliance with the then applicable fixed charge coverage ratio on a pro forma basis." — [10-K Note 13 p.79](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm). The FY2024 version also sprang 91 days before the 2027 notes matured; that limb is gone — [FY2024 10-K Note 15](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683825000008/uis-20241231.htm)
- Financial covenant: "The company is required to maintain a minimum fixed charge coverage ratio if the availability under the Amended and Restated ABL Credit Facility falls below the greater of 10% of the lenders' commitments under the facility and $12.5 million." Compliance: "the company had met all covenants and conditions under its various lending agreements. The company expects to continue to meet these covenants and conditions through at least the next twelve months." — [10-K Note 13 pp.76, 79](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- 2025 refinancing: tender offer at $1,006.25 per $1,000 (includes $30.00 early tender premium); aggregate paid 488.6 (including 3.0 early tender premium and 5.5 accrued interest and other expenses) for 480.1 principal; remaining 4.9 discharged by depositing U.S. government securities and redeemed; loss on extinguishment 7.0 (4.0 write off of costs and other expenses plus 3.0 premium); issuance costs paid 14.1. Proceeds also funded part of the pension deficit (200 of the 250 discretionary contribution) — [10-K Note 13 pp.77-79; Note 15 p.83; cash flow p.54](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- Ratings per risk factors: S&P B (cut from B+ in 2024), Moody's B2 (cut from B1 in 2025), both stable — [10-K Item 1A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)
- Other claims: operating lease liabilities 46.6, finance leases 41.2, standby letters of credit and surety bonds about 234 — [10-K Item 7 Other Commitments](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000074683826000006/uis-20251231.htm)

### Inferences
- Availability trigger: 10% of 125.0 is 12.5, so the test level is 12.5; availability of 92.2 is far above it, so on stated numbers the ratio test was not live at year end. The 10-K does not say so in terms.
- 125.0 less 13.5 of letters of credit would be 111.5; the stated availability is 92.2, so the receivables borrowing base, not the commitment, was the binding limit. The base itself is not disclosed.
- Springing trigger vs disclosed contributions: the largest estimate for the U.S. in the 10-K is about 47 in 2026, well below 100.0. The 2028 to 2030 estimate (241 global in aggregate) is not split by plan or year, so a U.S. year above 100 cannot be ruled out from the filing.
- No maturity of note before 2031: scheduled maturities are 5.4 or less a year through 2030.

### Gaps
- ABL borrowing base amount, actual fixed charge coverage ratio and its minimum level, and any covenant headroom number are not in the 10-K.
- The sentence "no significant debt maturities until 2031" does not appear in the 10-K (also absent from the FY2024 10-K and the Q4 2025 release). It is a later earnings release phrase; the 10-K supports it only through the maturity table.
- Exact redemption premium percentages after 15 Jan 2028 sit in the indenture, not the 10-K.
