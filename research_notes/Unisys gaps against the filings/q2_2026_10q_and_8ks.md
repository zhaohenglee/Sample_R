## 2031 notes indenture and ABL amendment terms

### Takeaway
The notes are second lien on ABL collateral and first lien on everything else, guaranteed by three US subsidiaries, callable at 105.313 from January 2028, with a 101% change of control put. The ABL matures 27 June 2030 and has a springing maturity tied to the 2031 notes and to US pension payments above $100M to the PBGC.

### Cited Findings
- Terms from the 8-K: $700.0M, 10.625%, interest 15 January and 15 July from 15 January 2026, maturity 15 January 2031, issued under an indenture dated 27 June 2025 with Computershare Trust Company, N.A. as trustee and collateral trustee. Guarantors: Unisys Holding Corporation, Unisys AP Investment Company I and Unisys NPL, Inc. Ranking: equal to senior debt; "structurally subordinated to all existing and future liabilities (including preferred stock, trade payables and pension liabilities) of the subsidiaries ... that are not Subsidiary Guarantors." Security: "liens on substantially all assets of Unisys and the Subsidiary Guarantors, other than certain excluded assets"; liens on certain ABL collateral are subordinated to the ABL lenders — [8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000110465925063658/tm2517689d2_8k.htm). The closing release adds "a pledge of 100% of the capital stock of each first tier domestic and foreign subsidiary" and says future guarantors will include every US subsidiary that guarantees the ABL — [Ex 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000110465925063658/tm2517689d2_ex99-1.htm). The intercreditor amendment removed the 65% limit on foreign stock pledges. The July 2025 deck describes the lien order as "a first lien on such assets constituting non-ABL Priority Collateral ... and a second lien on such assets constituting ABL Priority Collateral" — [July 2025 deck](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000110465925070218/tm2521530d1_ex99-1.htm).
- Optional redemption (Indenture Section 3.7): before 15 January 2028 at 100.0% plus the Applicable Premium (make whole); from 15 January 2028 at 105.313%, from 15 January 2029 at 102.656%, from 15 January 2030 at 100.000%; up to 40% with equity offering proceeds at 110.625% before 15 January 2028 (at least 60% must remain outstanding, within 120 days); up to 10% of principal per calendar year at 103% before 15 January 2028 — [Indenture Ex 4.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000110465925063658/tm2517689d2_ex4-1.htm).
- Change of control put (Section 4.13): offer within 30 days "at a purchase price equal to 101% of their principal amount plus accrued and unpaid interest". Triggers include any person or group obtaining at least 50% of voting power, sale of substantially all assets, or stockholder adoption of a liquidation plan. Asset sale proceeds must be offered at par.
- Debt incurrence (Section 4.9): general ratio debt only if Consolidated Coverage Ratio is "not less than 2.00 to 1.00"; permitted baskets include credit facilities up to the greater of $175.0M and the Borrowing Base, and pari passu debt up to Total Net Leverage "less than or equal to 1.70 to 1.00" plus the greater of $150.0M and 8.0% of Consolidated Total Assets. The July 2025 investor deck says 2.00x for the leverage test, which conflicts with the indenture text of 1.70x; the indenture controls.
- Restricted payments (Section 4.7): bars dividends, buybacks, non permitted investments and early repayment of subordinated debt unless no default, $1.00 of ratio debt capacity exists, and the cumulative total is within 50% of cumulative Consolidated Net Income from 1 April 2025 (less 100% of any deficit) plus equity proceeds and other builders plus a $50.0M general amount; many specific exceptions in 4.7(b).
- Pension protection (Section 4.12): "in no event shall the Company permit any Pension Obligations to rank pari passu or senior in priority as to the Collateral with the Notes". The 8-K summary also lists a covenant on "certain prepayments in respect of pension obligations"; I did not locate a separate section for it in the indenture text by keyword search.
- Covenant suspension (Section 4.17): if the notes get investment grade ratings from two agencies and no default exists, the debt, restricted payment, asset sale and similar covenants fall away.
- ABL amendment (Amendment No. 3, 27 June 2025, Bank of America as agent): size $125.0M, letters of credit sublimit $40.0M, uncommitted accordion to $155.0M, maturity moved from October 2027 to June 2030. Definition of Maturity Date: "the earliest of (x) June 27, 2030, (y) any Springing Maturity Date, or (z) any other date on which the Commitments are reduced to zero or otherwise terminated". Springing maturity, verbatim (struck "2027" removed): "'Springing Maturity Date' means the date that is ninety-one (91) days prior to either (a) the maturity date of the Senior Secured Notes or (b) any date upon which net U.S. pension contributions (after giving effect to prepayments held on account with the PBGC) in an amount in excess of $100,000,000 are required to be paid to the PBGC unless, on any such date (x) Liquidity exceeds the aggregate outstanding principal balance of the Senior Secured Notes or the aggregate amount of such required net pension contributions, as applicable, and (y) the Payment Conditions are satisfied." — [ABL amendment Ex 10.3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746838/000110465925063658/tm2517689d2_ex10-3.htm) (the exhibit is a redline; I read it with the struck text removed).
- Payment Conditions: no default; "Liquidity is not less than $130,000,000"; and pro forma compliance with the Section 6.18 fixed charge covenant. "Liquidity" is Availability plus Unrestricted Cash on Hand. The fixed charge coverage covenant applies only while Availability is below the greater of $12.5M and 10.0% of the commitment; its level is 1.00 to 1.00 for 30 June 2026 and each later quarter (0.90 for the 31 December 2025 and 31 March 2026 quarters).

### Inferences
- The springing clause now points to the 2031 notes, whose own maturity (15 January 2031) falls after the ABL's fixed maturity of 27 June 2030, so prong (a) cannot bind before the fixed date. Only prong (b), US pension payments above $100M to the PBGC, can pull the ABL maturity forward, and even then only if Liquidity is below the payment amount or the Payment Conditions fail.
- The earlier note's "liquidity above $130M and pro forma fixed charge coverage test" is a correct summary of the Payment Conditions; the earlier "91 days before any date on which US pension contributions above $100.0M are required" is correct but omits the Liquidity versus payment amount test.

### Gaps
- Optional redemption ladder through 2030 in the Offering Memorandum: not needed, indenture read.
- Whether the FCCR was in a trigger state at 30 June 2026: not stated; availability of $99.7M is far above the $12.5M trigger, so no test applies (inference from two quoted figures).
