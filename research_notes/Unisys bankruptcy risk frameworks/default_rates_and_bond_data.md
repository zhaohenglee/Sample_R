## 1. Historical default rate tables by rating (S&P and Moody's)

### Takeaway
S&P's 2024 study (published 27 Mar 2025) gives a B category 1-year cumulative default rate of about 2.9%, 3-year about 10.5%, 5-year about 15.6% (1981 to 2024 pooled), per a third-party reproduction only. CCC/C is about 26.1%, 41.3%, 46.5%. I could not retrieve S&P's own table or any Moody's annual study table, so Moody's B2 and Caa horizons are a gap.

### Inferences
- For a quick conversion, a B issuer sits near 3% one-year, 10 to 16% three-year, 15 to 22% five-year cumulative default on S&P history, depending on sample period. Use a range, not a point, until the primary table is pulled.
- Unisys's B (S&P) is the middle notch of the B category. Notch rates (B+, B, B-) would be tighter or wider than the category numbers, but I found none.
- The S&P 2024 annual B default rate (1.72%) is below the long run pooled one-year average (about 2.9%), so 2024 was a benign year for B, while CCC/C ran near the long run level.
